## 3. Pair Roles

### 3.1 `defense_defect_engineer`

Recommended role:

- `blockguide` all-purpose lead exemplar
- domain-truth-density benchmark
- bottleneck-control loop benchmark

Why:

- it is the first pair explicitly re-closed as deployable `GREENPLUS`
- the live opening is dense and authority-bearing
- the 70-block loop remains readable as a repeatable control engine, not a one-off spike

### 3.2 `office_checkup_next_day`

Recommended role:

- cleanest first-block conversion benchmark
- easiest onboarding exemplar for blockguide pacing

Why:

- the opening chain is short, legible, and now declared in-contract
- it is the clearest quick explanation of `proof -> reevaluation -> token -> next gate`
- it is useful when teaching or testing opening cleanliness

### 3.3 `wuxia_heavenly_physician`

Recommended role:

- default wuxguide front exemplar
- high-pain recovery-control benchmark

Why:

- it gives immediate family coverage outside blockguide
- the opening converts pain into status and access quickly
- the late-run repair is already closed, so it no longer carries whole-run drag debt

### 3.4 `투자물_골든_카나리아 테스트_canonical_v1`

Recommended role:

- market-power / macro-calendar specialist exemplar

Why:

- it is strongest when the operator wants investment-regression timing, calendar preemption, and capital rotation
- it is less useful than `office_checkup_next_day` for generic opening-pacing demonstration, but stronger for macro-market proof

### 3.5 `pantech_cyworld_reborn`

Recommended role:

- authority-ticket benchmark exemplar
- tech / platform turnaround specialist exemplar

Why:

- its access-right and ownership-token chain is the cleanest in the shelf
- use it when the audience cares about power-gate accumulation rather than pure pacing pedagogy

### 3.6 `chaebol_ent_empire`

Recommended role:

- entertainment-business texture exemplar
- people-placement / package-strategy loop exemplar

Why:

- it is strongest when the pitch needs talent placement, asymmetry, and package construction
- it is valuable, but more lane-specific than the default core 3
