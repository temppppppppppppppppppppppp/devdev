## 1. Executive Summary

The 4-lane survey converges on a clear ownership split:

- **Bible**: authoritative owner of admiration principles
- **Blueprint**: authoritative owner of admiration execution
- **Arc**: secondary owner that distributes admiration modes
- **Manuscript**: secondary renderer, and often too late for structural repair

The core diagnosis is:

`주인공 고평가` should not be treated as a raw number-reaction problem.
It should be designed as a differentiated admiration system built on:
- condition
- method
- risk
- information asymmetry
- ripple / social shock

## 2. Merged Owner Map

| Layer | Role | Merge conclusion |
|------|------|------------------|
| Bible | defines what deserves admiration | **authoritative** |
| Arc | distributes which admiration mode dominates a given arc | **secondary** |
| Blueprint | turns admiration into scenes, observer allocation, reveal order, POV staging | **authoritative for execution** |
| Manuscript | renders tone, pacing, understatement, reaction texture | **secondary / too late for core design** |

## 3. Highest-Signal Findings

### F-1. Bible must define the admiration axes

The bible layer is the correct place to define:
- what kinds of protagonist excellence matter in this work
- what reaction tiers different observers are allowed to have
- which praise patterns are forbidden

The strongest merged principle is:

Do not let the work default to `big number -> wow`.

Instead, define praise axes such as:
- impossible method from weak conditions
- abnormal risk tolerance under constraint
- structural / strategic reading of the board
- only-informed-observer recognition
- social hierarchy shock

This came through most strongly in T1.

### F-2. Blueprint is where admiration actually becomes legible

Blueprint is the point of maximum leverage for execution.

It owns:
- who notices
- who misunderstands
- where POV shifts happen
- how information gaps are staged
- when admiration arrives as fear, unease, or recognition instead of direct hype

Without blueprint staging, manuscript tends to fall back to generic explicit praise or raw-number astonishment.

This came through most strongly in T3.

### F-3. Arc should distribute admiration modes, not invent them

Arc is not the place to define what admiration means.
Arc is the place to rotate and distribute it.

In merged form:
- Bible defines the admiration-mode catalog
- Arc chooses which mode dominates which arc
- Blueprint executes that mode scene by scene

This came through most strongly in T2.

### F-4. Manuscript is too late for core admiration design

Manuscript can improve:
- POV contrast
- reaction tone
- information withholding texture
- understated fear / respect

But manuscript cannot safely invent:
- who should understand
- what the admiration mode is
- what information gap exists
- what social threshold is being crossed

If upstream layers do not define those, manuscript-level fixes become ad hoc.

This came through most strongly in T4.

## 4. Anti-Patterns To Avoid

The survey bundle also converged on what to avoid:

- `big_number_wow`
- uniform reaction from all observers
- narrator hype instead of staged inference
- instant recognition with no timing gap
- praise detached from class context or observer competence

These belong upstream as forbidden patterns, not as manuscript-only polish notes.

## 5. Best Next Design Shape

If this topic is operationalized later, the best order is:

1. **Bible-first definition wave**
   - define admiration axes
   - define observer tiers
   - define forbidden praise patterns
   - define threshold logic for when admiration is valid

2. **Blueprint execution wave**
   - add prompt-level staging guidance
   - push admiration away from number-size praise
   - explicitly use observer allocation, POV, reveal timing, and information asymmetry

3. **Arc distribution follow-up**
   - rotate admiration modes by arc
   - prevent repetitive admiration style

4. **Manuscript rendering follow-up only if needed**
   - tighten prose-level rendering after upstream design is stable

## 6. Immediate Recommendation

Do **not** start at manuscript.
Do **not** start with arc-only distribution.

If a future wave is opened, the first move should be:

`Bible-level protagonist_evaluation design definition`

Then:

`Blueprint-level admiration staging guidance`

## 7. Merge Conclusion

The owner question is resolved.

- Bible owns the principles.
- Blueprint owns the execution.
- Arc distributes.
- Manuscript renders.

The problem is not “how to make people say the protagonist is amazing.”
The problem is “how to design differentiated admiration modes so that the work earns high-evaluation without collapsing into numeric hype.”

## 8. Mandatory Final Lines
