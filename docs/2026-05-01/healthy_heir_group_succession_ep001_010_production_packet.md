## 1. Production Lock

User pacing lock:

- Webnovel pacing must be fast.
- The first reader-facing block is `EP002-EP006`.
- `EP002-EP006` must not spend all its force on only one incident.
- Required opening double incident:
  - main incident: southern cold warehouse closure hold
  - additional incident: PF guarantee auto-extension notice / bank deadline / collateral-date mismatch

Protagonist lock:

- Seo Do-yoon is self-interested and efficiency-first.
- He does not need to be kind or cruel.
- His rule is: if it preserves rights, leverage, skilled labor, certification, time, or authority, it is worth saving.
- Employee protection is never a moral speech. It must be framed as skilled-labor, certification, contract-right, or negotiation leverage preservation.

No-fantasy lock:

- no system window
- no value UI
- no supernatural appraisal
- no prophecy speech
- regression memory guides suspicion only
- present documents win the room

## 3. EP001: 서명 직전

Function:

- Cold open and protagonist definition.
- Do not spend the first full win here.
- The episode exists to show that Do-yoon is not a sentimental rescuer.

Opening image:

- Future liquidation memory.
- Hanseo Group assets sold in pieces.
- Disposal line: `Southern Cold Warehouse - sold after closure`.
- Present cut: Seo Moon-seok's pen above the closure approval line.

Core scenes:

- Do-yoon wakes into the present timeline.
- He remembers the collapse chain: cold warehouse closure -> PF auto-extension failure -> rating pressure -> production-right loss.
- He enters the chairman-room meeting at the moment of signature.
- He asks for original P&L and contract appendix, not belief.

Do-yoon attitude:

- He is not there to save everyone.
- He wants the first minute of authority.
- His first line should feel efficient, not emotional.

Suggested line:

`서명은 하셔도 됩니다. 다만 빠진 열 하나만 보고 하시죠.`

End hook:

- Seo Moon-seok's pen stops.
- CFO's tablet receives a PF guarantee auto-extension notice, but no one treats it as related yet.

Must avoid:

- Do-yoon explaining the future.
- Family melodrama.
- Chairman instantly trusting him.

## 13. Scene-Level Pacing Rules

Each episode must include:

- one document/object entering the page
- one rational resistance
- one Do-yoon efficiency move
- one same-episode receipt or usable setback
- one ending hook that opens the next episode

Do not spend more than two scenes on explanation before a document or counterparty pressure appears.

Preferred scene rhythm:

- Scene 1: pressure appears
- Scene 2: document/object reframes it
- Scene 3: resistance reacts
- Scene 4: Do-yoon extracts authority, access, or a usable card
- Scene 5: next pressure enters

## 14. Forbidden Drifts

Hard fail:

- EP002-EP006 only discusses cold warehouse closure with no PF incident
- Do-yoon saves employees because he is nice
- chairman is emotionally moved and gives broad authority
- CFO or logistics president acts stupid
- future memory is treated as proof
- closure fully resolves by EP006
- reward is praise only
- no same-episode receipt

Hard pass:

- EP002 puts PF notice into the same memo
- EP003 makes bid qualification and PF deadline visible together
- EP004 uses failure condition to buy narrow authority
- EP005 converts blame into recoverable rights and confirms PF date mismatch
- EP006 gives official closure hold plus PF emergency agenda
