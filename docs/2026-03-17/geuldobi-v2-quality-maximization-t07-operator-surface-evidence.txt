- Fact: packaged desktop files are constrained to `src/**/*`; root-level Electron shadow-entry drift is explicitly regression-tested.
- Fact: approved live network/control-plane surfaces split into splash `fetch /status`, main-window `WebSocket /events`, main-process bridge fetches for `/run`, `/stop`, `/status`, `/quality/summary`, `/quality/dashboard`, `/safe-ops/preview`, `/quality/review`, `/run/{run_id}/input`, plus one renderer-direct Gemini key validation fetch to `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models`.

- Fact: splash readiness is shallow and transport-centric; `geuldobi-desktop/src/splash/splash.js` polls `/status` until `state == "idle"` and does not inspect proof or quality readiness.
- Fact: websocket runtime schema is limited to `run_started`, `stdout`, `prompt_request`, `prompt_resolved`, `prompt_timeout`, `run_completed`, `run_failed`, and `run_stopped`; there is no structured progress or gate-semantics event type in `docs/implementation/event-schema-v1.json`.
