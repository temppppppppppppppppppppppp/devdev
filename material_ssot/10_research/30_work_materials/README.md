# 30_work_materials

Role:

- hold work-level normalized research packs
- bridge raw research and preprocess-side evidence into a reusable handoff contract
- give `20_pitch`, `30_stage0_preprocess`, and `40_phase0_design` one place to read the same material truth

Pack shape:

- `00_sources/source-inventory.md`
- `10_user_agreements.md`
- `20_fact_lock.json`
- `30_domain_map.md`
- `40_narrative_seed_bank.json`
- `50_pattern_refs.md`
- `60_gap_log.md`
- `90_material_pack.json`

Operating rule:

- raw corpus can stay in legacy or deferred buckets
- the normalized pack is the stage-facing truth for a specific `work_id`
- each new pack should be assembled from confirmed evidence only
- unresolved raw corpus or domain gaps must stay explicit in `60_gap_log.md`
