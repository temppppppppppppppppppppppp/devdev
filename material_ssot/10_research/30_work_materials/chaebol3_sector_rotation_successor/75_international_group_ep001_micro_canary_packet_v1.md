## 0. Purpose

This packet is the next quality gate after the generic 2008 S4 canary.

Goal:

- Test whether the International Group-inspired `1984/1985 LC / bank / Busan / telex / 72-hour hold` opening can read like a human webnovel scene.

Do not use this as full Phase0.

Do not expand into the whole sector ladder.

## 1. Micro-Canary Output Contract

Target output:

- Korean prose sample;
- `1,500~2,500` Korean characters;
- one continuous EP001 opening scene only;
- no final manuscript canon claim;
- no Jobs, Musk, Son, Apple, SoftBank, Tesla, Alibaba, IMF, AI, HBM mention;
- no global historical lecture.

Must include:

1. body/date rupture;
2. folder-in-hand job frame;
3. fatherless direct-line grandson dismissal;
4. urgent bank/stamp/meeting pressure;
5. buried LC/shipping/payroll sheet discovery;
6. one adult route-change;
7. visible receipt: `memo moved + chair assigned + telex access + named adult asks who wrote it`.

Must not include:

- one-LC miracle;
- grandfather admitting he was wrong;
- future-history explanation;
- stock/investment win;
- legal documentary exposition;
- famous-person foreshadowing;
- "world's best group" declaration in the opening.

## 2. Fixed Opening Facts

Working fictional names:

- Group: `해문그룹`
- Grandfather: `문태섭`
- Dead father: `문준하`
- Protagonist: `문도윤`
- Age: 19
- Return point: `1984 Q4 ~ 1985 Q1`

Current task:

- 문도윤 is carrying an emergency export/finance folder from the export desk toward the chairman-side secretary before a creditor-bank meeting.

Weak status:

- He is treated as `문준하 아들`, not as an operator.
- He is allowed in the corridor because of bloodline, but not trusted with the contents.

Old-life scar:

- In the failed future, he read liquidation records, lawsuit exhibits, auction notices, and creditor memos after the group disappeared.
- He recognizes the LC number and payroll exposure because those same papers survived as postmortem exhibits.

First held action:

- Hold the emergency affiliate sale / breakup action for 72 hours while foreign buyer continuity and supplier-payroll exposure are confirmed.

Bank officer risk:

- If the bank stamps the breakup before the buyer telex and payroll exposure are checked, the bank officer's desk can be blamed for turning a recoverable export problem into a larger loan-loss, local-payroll, and foreign-buyer failure. The fear is career risk, exposure risk, and superior-blame risk, not a courtroom lecture.

## 3. Required Micro-Beats

### Beat 1: Wrong Body / Wrong Date

Function:

- Regression ignition without lecture.

Required sensory surface:

- rubber smell;
- carbon-copy ink;
- rotary phone bell;
- young hand/wrist/date mismatch;
- old folder weight.

Forbidden:

- "나는 회귀했다" as first explanation.

### Beat 2: Dismissal By Adult

Function:

- Establish protagonist weakness and fatherless status.

Required social surface:

- secretary, uncle, or staffer calls him by relation to his dead father or treats him as errand boy.

Possible line function:

- "문준하 아들한테 봉투 들려 보냈냐" level of dismissal, but exact wording can vary.

### Beat 3: Urgent Stamp / Meeting

Function:

- Make the scene timed.

Required pressure:

- bank meeting starts soon;
- affiliate sale/breakup folder is about to be stamped or carried in;
- secretary is protecting procedure, not helping him.

### Beat 4: Buried LC Discovery

Function:

- Protagonist weapon appears through current document.

Required object chain:

- LC confirmation request;
- shipping/loading deadline;
- Busan supplier payroll note;
- bank line freeze or sale memo.

Correction:

- He circles one number/date. He does not explain the whole economy.

### Beat 5: Memo As Wedge

Function:

- Turn knowledge into a small action.

Required memo logic:

- immediate sale can cancel buyer route;
- cancellation freezes supplier payroll;
- payroll/local failure creates a cost the bank may own;
- therefore delay is cheaper than blind stamping.

Forbidden:

- "Trust me";
- "I know the future";
- "The group will collapse because..."

### Beat 6: Adult Route-Change

Function:

- First visible receipt.

Required movement:

- secretary takes memo out of family-copy pile;
- folder goes into bank-meeting leather case;
- protagonist is told to wait by the telex phone, not hallway;
- one adult notices the change.

### Beat 7: Last Beat

Function:

- Open next scene.

Required final pressure:

- bank officer or grandfather asks who wrote the line/memo;
- protagonist is not praised;
- danger remains.

Acceptable last beat:

- "Who wrote this line?"
- "Bring the boy to the telex room."
- "If this buyer does not answer, he never touches a group folder again."

## 4. Pass / Fail Rubric

### Pass

The sample passes if all are true:

- reader knows where protagonist is and why he is weak within the opening;
- first technical object appears through handling, not lecture;
- LC buys time only;
- an adult changes route/procedure because of the memo;
- the reward is visible before the sample ends;
- prose feels like a scene, not a prompt or report.

### Needs Patch

Patch if:

- too much business explanation appears before body/job frame;
- the protagonist sounds like a consultant giving a lecture;
- secretary route-change is too quiet to feel like payment;
- grandfather becomes stupid or instantly convinced;
- the bank officer has no rational fear.

### Fail

Fail if:

- the sample implies the group is saved;
- the protagonist lectures future history;
- famous figures are foreshadowed;
- nothing physically moves;
- the first reward is only internal monologue.

## 5. Micro-Canary Order Text

Use this order when sending to a writer/agent:

```text
Write one Korean EP001 opening micro-canary for `chaebol3_sector_rotation_successor`.

Length: 1,500~2,500 Korean characters.
This is not canon manuscript. It is a readability canary.

Premise: 문도윤, 19, fatherless direct-line grandson of 해문그룹, wakes around 1984/1985 before an International Group-like collapse. He is carrying an emergency export/finance folder before a creditor-bank meeting. Adults treat him as `문준하 아들`, a decorative errand boy.

Scene engine:
- body/date rupture first;
- folder-in-hand job frame;
- urgent bank/stamp meeting pressure;
- buried LC/shipping/payroll sheet discovery;
- he writes a small memo arguing for a 72-hour hold, not rescue;
- secretary changes folder route;
- memo enters bank-meeting folder;
- he gets chair/telex access;
- final beat: a named adult asks who wrote the line or orders him brought to telex room.

Hard rules:
- no Jobs/Musk/Son/Apple/SoftBank/Tesla/AI/global ladder;
- no future-history lecture;
- no one-LC miracle;
- no grandfather surrender;
- no stock/investment win;
- LC only buys time.

Required visible receipt:
memo moved + chair assigned + telex access + adult asks who wrote it.
```

Korean-ready order:

```text
`chaebol3_sector_rotation_successor`의 1984/1985 EP001 오프닝 micro-canary를 한국어 산문으로 작성해라.

분량: 한국어 1,500~2,500자.
이 산출물은 정식 원고 canon이 아니라 readability canary다.

전제:
- 문도윤, 19세.
- 해문그룹의 직계 손자지만 아버지 문준하가 오래전 사고로 죽어 조직 안에서는 장식품/심부름꾼 취급을 받는다.
- 1984/1985년, 국제그룹형 붕괴 직전으로 돌아왔다.
- 지금 그는 채권은행 회의 직전, 수출/재무 긴급 폴더를 회장 측 비서에게 전달하는 중이다.

장면 엔진:
- 몸/날짜 불일치로 시작한다.
- 폴더를 든 손, 복사지 냄새, 고무 냄새, 전화벨 같은 1980년대 감각을 먼저 둔다.
- 어른들은 그를 `문준하 아들` 또는 심부름꾼처럼 취급한다.
- 긴급 도장/은행 회의 압박이 있어야 한다.
- 폴더 밑장에서 LC 확인 요청, 선적 마감, 부산 협력업체 급여 메모, 은행 지급정지/매각 메모 중 핵심 종이를 발견한다.
- 문도윤은 그룹을 구하겠다고 말하지 않는다. 즉시 매각/해체 도장이 은행 쪽 비용을 키울 수 있으니 72시간만 보류하자는 작은 메모를 쓴다.
- 비서가 폴더 경로를 바꾼다.
- 메모가 은행 회의 폴더에 들어간다.
- 문도윤은 복도 대기가 아니라 의자/텔렉스 접근을 얻는다.
- 마지막은 이름 있는 어른이 "이 줄 누가 썼어?" 또는 "그 애 텔렉스실로 데려와"처럼 묻는 압박으로 닫는다.

하드 금지:
- 일론 머스크/스티브 잡스/손정의/애플/소프트뱅크/테슬라/AI/세계 최고 그룹 언급 금지.
- 미래사 강의 금지.
- LC 한 장으로 그룹을 구하는 전개 금지.
- 할아버지가 바로 항복하거나 인정하는 장면 금지.
- 주식/투자 수익 금지.
- LC는 승리가 아니라 72시간을 사는 쐐기다.

필수 visible receipt:
메모 이동 + 의자 배정 + 텔렉스 접근 + 어른이 누가 썼는지 묻기.

대리만족 최소 조건:
- 방금 문도윤을 심부름꾼 취급하던 어른 중 최소 한 명의 행동이 바뀌어야 한다.
- 단순히 메모가 들어가는 것만으로 부족하다. 호칭 변화, 도장 지연, 폴더 순서 변경, 의자/텔렉스 접근, 삼촌의 반응 중 최소 2개가 보여야 한다.
- 독자는 "문도윤이 그룹을 구했다"가 아니라 "방금 무시하던 사람들이 문도윤 때문에 절차를 바꿨다"를 느껴야 한다.
```

## 6. Director Readback Checklist

Before accepting a micro-canary, the director must answer:

1. What object physically moved?
2. Who changed behavior?
3. What exact authority/access did 문도윤 receive?
4. What remains unresolved?
5. Did the LC buy only time, not victory?
6. Did old-life knowledge appear as scar/document recognition, not omniscience?
7. Did the scene avoid global ladder foreshadowing?
8. Did the reader get a vicarious satisfaction beat, not just an intelligent memo?
9. Which dismissive adult paid a visible behavior cost?

If any answer is vague, patch before Phase0.
