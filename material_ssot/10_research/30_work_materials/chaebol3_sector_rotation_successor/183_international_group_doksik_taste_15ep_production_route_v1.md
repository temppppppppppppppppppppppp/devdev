## 2. How To Make 15 Episodes That Feel Like Real Manuscript

The 15 episodes should be made in three story tranches:

1. EP001-EP005: grandfather, money, dedicated team, first bet, first adult silence.
2. EP006-EP010: family line notices him, team becomes loyal, school/time compression becomes a weapon.
3. EP011-EP015: first outside adult board, first affiliate name, bigger capital, next-era smell.

Each tranche must be written as story material before it becomes a Firefly input.

The material must answer in story language:

- What does Doyun want this episode?
- Who still thinks he is just a child?
- What amount of money, person, room, schedule, or family standing moves?
- Who changes their tone because of what happened?
- What larger adult world opens by the ending?

If the answer sounds like a system label, rewrite it.

## 3. Fifteen-Episode Spine

EP001: Doyun returns from the ruined future as a seventeen-year-old grandson. He goes to his grandfather and asks for money, two adults, and a room at Haemun Securities. The point is not permission. The point is that the grandfather stops laughing.

EP002: The dedicated team is assigned like babysitting duty. Doyun treats them like the first people of his future empire. He gives secrecy, performance pay, and a first market order. The team leader begins to doubt the babysitting frame.

EP003: The first bet starts moving. Adults still talk about school, exams, and manners, but Doyun uses that topic to compress time: 검정고시, SAT, overseas route, and early group entry. The team stays late because the numbers are no longer funny.

EP004: Money arrives. Not "a clever child" money, but an amount that changes the room. Bonuses go out. The grandfather feels pride and unease together. The team starts watching Doyun's mouth before the phone rings.

EP005: Doyun opens the board beyond Korea: Japan, Middle East, America, Seoul Olympics, semiconductors, telecom. The grandfather brings him into an overseas schedule. The family still thinks this is a strange indulgence; the adults near the money know it is not.

EP006: A cousin or uncle line notices the private money-room. Their first reaction is not villainy but irritation: why is the chairman wasting attention on a boy? Doyun lets them underestimate the wrong thing.

EP007: Doyun chooses one overlooked person differently from the old life. The person is not instantly loyal; they are tired, cautious, and practical. Doyun wins them with money timing and a future that sounds useful.

EP008: The grandfather gives a second test. It is not a lecture. It is a meeting, a call, or a schedule where Doyun must behave like a grandson and a businessman at once.

EP009: The team stops being assigned staff and becomes Doyun's people. Not through slogans: through a bonus, a saved reputation, a night of work that only makes sense if they believe him.

EP010: School is resolved as compression, not slice-of-life. Doyun makes adults accept that he is not avoiding school; he is buying time before the next era opens.

EP011: The first outside figure appears: a banker, overseas contact, trading man, or foreign partner who cannot be impressed by family name alone. Doyun wins one serious look.

EP012: The family line pushes back. They try to frame Doyun as a spoiled heir or a chairman's toy. Doyun does not argue much. The money and the person he has moved answer for him.

EP013: The first affiliate name becomes possible. Doyun is no longer just moving money in a room; he is touching the group's future shape.

EP014: Bigger capital enters. The danger is also bigger. Someone who laughed now tries to stand beside him; someone who smiled now starts calculating against him.

EP015: The next era is smelled, not fully explained. Doyun sees the thing others treat as distant noise: Japan, telecom, semiconductors, overseas capital, or a person who will matter. The ending should feel like the board got wider.

## 4. Translation Into Firefly

After the 15-episode plan passes taste audit:

1. Create or revise BI from the human plan.
   - Family, group, grandfather, Doyun, team, rivals, money calendar, and long-range promise.
2. Create TR only as much as needed for the next tested tranche.
   - Start with EP001-EP005 or EP001-EP010 equivalent.
   - Do not multiply to full detailed 70 until prose proves the taste.
3. Run Firefly S2-S3-S4.
   - S2 turns the plan into arc units.
   - S3 turns arc units into episode blueprints.
   - S4 writes actual manuscript.
4. Audit every 5 episodes against real-reader pleasure.
   - Did money, people, family standing, or adult tone change?
   - Did Doyun feel like a young heir with old-life vision, not a young consultant?
   - Did the episode give a reason to pay for the next one?
