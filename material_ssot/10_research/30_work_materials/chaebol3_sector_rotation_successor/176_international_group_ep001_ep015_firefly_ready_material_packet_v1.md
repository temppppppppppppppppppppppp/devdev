## 2. Writer-Facing Episode Payload

### EP001 - Stamp Does Not Land

Start in the export-finance corridor outside the creditor-bank meeting. Mun Doyun wakes in his young body with the emergency folder edge cutting into his hand, the 1985 date stamp visible, and adults calling him `Mun Junha's son` rather than a decision-maker.

The disposal cover hides L/C confirmation, shipping deadline, and Busan payroll memo. He does not explain future history. He writes one narrow 72-hour verification hold line. The payment is not trust. The payment is physical: disposal cover moves back, ink pad closes, approval sheet turns sideways, and the bank man asks who wrote the line.

Resists: Park Yunjae protects meeting accident risk; Mun Seongpil protects clean-sale momentum; Kim Taewan protects bank exposure.

End on the pending line, not triumph.

### EP002 - Watched Telex Chair

Start with distrust. The old chairman and bank-side man do not believe Doyun. The blank approval line remains blank.

Doyun asks for one outgoing telex and 72 hours only. He accepts a failure condition: if buyer continuity and Busan payroll exposure do not prove out, he loses corridor, folder, sales-office, and document access.

Payment: Park Yunjae writes the telex-room chair and supervision condition. The chair opens, but watched.

Resists: chairman protects founder authority; bank protects its record trail; Seongpil protects the sale route.

End with the chair and the condition, not praise.

### EP003 - Outside Evidence Gets Clipped

Start at the telex/export desk, not the family room. Export staff blocks the outgoing telex because department name can be blamed.

Doyun asks the buyer only about shipment continuity, cancellation cost, and reply deadline. Then he calls Busan payroll/accounting. Han Gicheol is hostile and factual: wage date, unpaid amount, shipment quantity, stoppage trigger.

Payment: buyer reply and Busan payroll fax are clipped beside Kim Taewan's pending approval line. Family argument loses monopoly over the answer.

End with the outside facts attached to the bank line.

### EP004 - First Task, Not Authority

Start after the crisis note already exists. Do not reopen it as a new reward.

Mun Taeseop gives Doyun one task under Park Yunjae supervision: prove which shipment/payment route can stay alive for 72 hours without making bank exposure worse. Seongpil tries to reduce the job to harmless document preparation. Kim Taewan demands fields that will survive bank review.

Payment: a signed task sheet, waiting-room phone route, restricted file route, three document bundles, and fixed report times.

End with the task sheet still open and the next clock visible.

### EP005 - Busan Pay Window

Move to Busan. Show empty pay envelopes, workers near the pay window, the factory board, one shipment line that may die today, and a defect tray no one wants to own.

The local manager protects Busan's face. The factory-side man protects himself from Seoul blame. Nobody wants an unknown heir's name on a public notice.

Doyun removes his own name from the posted pay notice. He separates first pay from product-quality truth.

Payment: the board gets a 40 percent first-pay notice, one shipment line stays alive, and the defect tray becomes the next condition.

End with the notice still on the board.

### EP006 - Product In Hand

Keep the camera on hands and shoes. Use a quality table, sample shoes, defect tray, limited spec sheet, and passed-count payment rule.

The quality lead protects credibility. The factory manager wants quantity because lost quantity becomes his blame.

Doyun cuts the shiny defect-prone variant and keeps the plain line that survives the hand. He lets failed sample, pass count, and payment rule do the work.

Payment: the plain line remains in the box, shiny line is cut, money is tied to passed samples.

End with the limited spec signed under objection.

### EP007 - Buyer Desk

Put the sample box on a distributor or school-supply buyer's desk. The buyer sees the Haemun mark as risk and asks about return rate, parent calls, delivery penalty, and shelf space before opening the box.

Doyun lowers exposure. A bigger ask becomes a tiny trial quantity under buyer terms. He offers return cap and support windows.

Payment: the sample box is not returned. It remains beside the buyer's phone and return ledger, not on the front shelf.

End with the box staying on the wrong part of the desk.

### EP008 - School Gym Opens

Put boxes into the school gym and equipment room. The teacher protects students, complaint risk, and her own name on school paper. No photos.

Doyun does not turn this into a promo event. He cuts opened quantity and lets the teacher control student-facing words.

Payment: opened pairs are limited, the Haemun name enters temporary equipment paper, and one clear watch condition is written.

End with the name in public but under teacher custody.

### EP009 - Complaint Does Not Kill The Name

Start from the heel-rub complaint. Do not hide it and do not call the trial a success.

The teacher records the discomfort. Buyer and distributor protect return ledger, parent calls, and shelf space. Doyun separates discomfort from full product failure and narrows the next ask.

Payment: one complaint is handled on page, remaining boxes are not returned, and the public memo can be attached to finance without lying.

End with messy proof, not clean applause.

### EP010 - Finance Room And File Hour

Move to a small finance room, not the first crisis room. Bring distributor paper, school memo, pay notice, spec sheet, and defect note.

Seongpil attacks the result as too small. Finance protects budget precedent and file-room hierarchy.

Doyun asks for less: 100,000 won, one hour, three copies, no original movement, import-material file scope.

Payment: finance marks review, the file-room card moves under custody, and the import-material file reaches the desk.

End with the clock running.

### EP011 - File Contents, Not Card

Start inside the file-room. The card is old news.

Use cost sheet, shipment ledger, swatch index, and use-right note. The clerk turns pages and protects originals. Seongpil mocks the size of the inquiry.

Doyun does not ask for a budget. He finds a mismatch: heel-counter strip, adhesive sample, shipment route, and school basic line use-right.

Payment: one supplier/material question leaves the file-room on a copied sheet. One copy remains unused for the trading desk.

End with the question, not the card.

### EP012 - Trading Desk Inquiry

Move to the trading desk. The department stamp is in a drawer. Phone-telex time is limited.

The trading section chief protects department name, supplier relationship, and foreign-currency quote trail. He does not want an heir's guess leaving as purchase intent.

Doyun narrows the ask: compatibility and sample availability only. No purchase, no price negotiation, no big order.

Payment: an outgoing inquiry is filed under finance review with a reply deadline.

End with the inquiry leaving in a smaller name than Doyun wants.

### EP013 - Sample Custody

Move to the sample locker because supplier reply is slow. The existing samples are old, rejected, or half-forgotten, but they can be tested today.

The custodian protects sample inventory, return tags, and department blame surface. Good samples are not released.

Doyun asks for three rejected strips and an adhesive lid sample under return tag. He argues that bad samples are more useful because the factory will not trust perfect samples.

Payment: three strips and a tiny adhesive sample leave custody under Park Yunseo's confirmation and Doyun's visible accompaniment.

End with the samples tied to a return tag.

### EP014 - Busan Line Test

Move back to Busan. Heat press, old last, wear bench, adhesive clock, and line schedule are visible.

Quality lead protects credibility. Factory manager protects schedule and blame. The test can fix heel rub and create a new defect.

Doyun splits variables: one old material control, one new reinforcement with old adhesive, one combined variant if allowed. The quality lead writes the defect table before the test.

Payment: not perfect product. Payment is a 30-pair test sheet with one known defect condition and one passed condition.

End with a test permission stamp, not a production change.

### EP015 - Public Retest And Catalog Door

Return to the public surface and then finance-sales. The earlier student complaint is retested under teacher/buyer restrictions. No photo, no applause.

If the same-size complaint improves, buyer still protects return deadline. Finance still protects budget creep. Sales tries to overclaim.

Doyun narrows again: repeat confirmation money for the changed reinforcement, Monday delivery condition, and only one catalog/license contents page for the next item family.

Payment: repeat confirmation fund plus catalog contents page. The next door is school sportswear / indoor shoe / basic sporting-goods licensing or public procurement.

End with the catalog contents page, not brand revival.
