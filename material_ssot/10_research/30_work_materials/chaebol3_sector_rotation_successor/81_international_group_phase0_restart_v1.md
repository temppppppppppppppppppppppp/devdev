## 3. Protagonist Lock

문도윤은 이런 인물이 아니다:

- a self-made outsider;
- a repentant wastrel;
- a stock-picking genius;
- a teenage consultant lecturing the economy.

He is:

- legitimate by blood but politically weak because his father is dead;
- tolerated in corridors, not trusted with contents;
- angry because the old life showed him the group did not merely fail, it was taken apart through specific papers no one protected;
- dangerous because he remembers liquidation records, lawsuit exhibits, auction notices, creditor memos, buyer cancellations, and payroll failures as scars.

His weapon:

`failed-future postmortem memory -> current document recognition -> narrow hold condition -> adult self-protection -> small access token`

His tone:

- efficient, not cold;
- polite enough not to get thrown out;
- self-owned enough not to hand the folder over just because an uncle says so;
- acts through conditions, not prophecy.
