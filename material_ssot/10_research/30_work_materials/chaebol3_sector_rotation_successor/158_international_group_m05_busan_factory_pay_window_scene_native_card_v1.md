Han Gicheol resists because Seoul writes dates but Busan receives faces. He must not become grateful. Factory manager Jang Mansik resists because a false 700-unit promise will become his blame. Quality lead Jo Youngmi resists because defect product will kill the next order.

## 5. Pass / Fail

Pass if:

- the scene leaves the room and lives in factory/pay-window objects;
- Busan resists Seoul for practical reasons;
- payment is partial and physical;
- no worker cheer or savior credit appears;
- product quality enters through defect tray or sample object;
- Doyun removes or avoids public heir credit;
- next gate becomes product-hand/spec, not another payroll checklist.

Fail if:

- payroll is fully solved;
- workers thank or rally around Doyun;
- factory adults become passive recipients;
- the scene pays only with another sheet;
- no product object appears;
- next gate returns to Seoul meeting.
