## 0. Purpose

This file turns the International Group-style historical atlas into usable planning gates.

Rule:

- A sector is not a topic.
- A sector is a door with a deadline, a paper/object surface, a resistant owner, a protagonist lever, and a reward token.

If EP001 canary passes, the new full planning document should draw from this matrix before any TR 1-10 generation.

## 1. Core Fiction Position

Re-plan premise:

- A fallen Korean conglomerate inspired by International/Kukje Group collapses around the 1985 window.
- The grandfather made the fatal judgment: he misread political pressure, creditor-bank dependency, export/FX exposure, and affiliate guarantees.
- The protagonist's father died long ago in an accident, leaving the grandson as an emotionally legitimate but institutionally weak successor.
- The protagonist dies after watching the name disappear, then returns to `1984 Q4 ~ 1985 Q1`.
- He grows the group from survival, to Korea top, to world-top industrial-investment group.

Reader pleasure:

- restore a stolen family name;
- make adults who dismissed him adopt his frame;
- turn historical windows into money, people, authority, and world access;
- meet giants through useful deal surfaces, not fan-service cameos.

## 3. Real-Person Gate Use

Real people can appear only when the protagonist has earned the meeting through a concrete surface:

- Steve Jobs / Apple: component, logistics, supplier qualification, retail/telecom route.
- Masayoshi Son / SoftBank: internet fund, Korea/Japan/China platform bridge, Alibaba-style co-invest.
- Elon Musk / PayPal/SpaceX/Tesla: payments network, battery/power electronics/composites, patient capital.
- Korean chaebol heads: supplier bottleneck, distressed asset deal, government/creditor room, component allocation.

Do not write:

- the protagonist lecturing Jobs on product taste;
- the protagonist telling Musk how rockets or EVs work;
- secret defamatory scenes about living public figures;
- puppet meetings where famous people exist only to praise the protagonist.

Correct pleasure:

- The protagonist is prepared.
- The other side is formidable.
- The room changes because the protagonist controls one material door they need.

## 4. First Planning Extraction

If canary passes, the first full planning document should not use all gates immediately.

Recommended first macro arcs:

1. `Save The Name`:
   - group survival;
   - Busan/export chain;
   - grandfather forced into crisis-cell concession.
2. `Make The Name Visible`:
   - footwear/sportswear;
   - Olympics-adjacent brand/public return;
   - one civic/hotel/trading prestige gate.
3. `Build The Technology Spine`:
   - semiconductor supplier;
   - CDMA/mobile;
   - broadband/data center seed.
4. `Buy The Crisis`:
   - IMF distressed assets;
   - bank workout legitimacy;
   - foreign investor credibility.
5. `Become The Global Counterparty`:
   - Apple;
   - SoftBank/Alibaba;
   - PayPal network;
   - Tesla/SpaceX.

TR 1-10 should probably cover only macro arc 1 plus the first bite of macro arc 2.

Do not compress the whole world-top rise into the opening.

## 5. Planning Guardrails

Avoid these flattenings:

- `new sector = new lecture`;
- `new historical event = protagonist buys stock`;
- `famous person appears = reward`;
- `numbers go up = authority`;
- `grandfather admits everything too early`;
- `every conflict occurs in chairman room`;
- `the father accident becomes melodrama dump`;
- `International Group reference becomes literal legal replay`.

Required per sector:

- one human group whose life/work changes;
- one resistant actor with rational fear;
- one paper/object that can move hands;
- one narrow protagonist action;
- one visible reward token;
- one next gate opened by that reward.
