## 3. What We Actually Learned

### Lesson 1 - Reward Is Object Position

The strongest receipts are not praise lines.

They are object positions:

- the task sheet stays open;
- the posted notice stays on the factory board;
- the plain sample remains in the box;
- the sample box stays beside the buyer's phone and return ledger;
- the school memo keeps `해문 기본형 운동화` below the equipment list;
- the import-material file reaches the 자료실 desk.

For S4, the handoff must specify object position at the end of every unit.

### Lesson 2 - Each Room Changes What Counts As Credit

The same action cannot mean the same thing everywhere.

- In the meeting room, having 문도윤's name inside the line is a reward.
- In the factory, removing his unknown name from the public pay notice makes the notice heavier.
- At the distributor desk, not getting front display is the only reason the sample survives.
- At school, not photographing the trial prevents promo smell and protects the teacher.
- In finance, the reward is not budget approval but one-hour controlled access.

This is the strongest anti-slop finding in the tranche.

### Lesson 3 - Competence Is Narrowing, Not Declaring

문도윤 is most human-like when he reduces the claim:

- one supervised task, not authority;
- 40% first pay, not payroll rescue;
- plain line, not flashy product;
- 30 trial pairs, not comeback display;
- 12 opened pairs, not full use;
- 100,000 won / one hour, not a budget win.

This produces commercial satisfaction because the smaller surface actually survives.

### Lesson 4 - Resistance Must Protect Something Real

The useful resisting actors each protect a different asset:

- 김태완: bank exposure and audit trail;
- 한기철: payroll credibility and Busan's face;
- 장만식: factory blame surface;
- 조영미: quality credibility;
- 배상록: shelf space, parent calls, return ledger;
- 최민정: students and complaint risk;
- 신동규: budget precedent and file-access hierarchy;
- 문성필: old control and anti-overclaim frame.

S4 must receive the resisting actor's protected asset, not only the actor's mood.

### Lesson 5 - Dissent Can Be Stronger Than Agreement

False agreement smells like AI.

The better receipts contain dissent:

- `검수 기준 확인. 장식선 제외 이견 있음.`
- `생산 일정 이견 있음.`
- buyer cuts 60 to 30;
- teacher records heel-rub complaint;
- finance marks `본 배정 아님`.

This should become a handoff rule: the document becomes heavier when a real objection remains written on it.

## 4. Closure Pattern Watch

The canaries repeatedly close with a compressed thesis line:

- "공장이 닫히지 않았다."
- "물건 하나가 아직 버텼다."
- "반품 장부 옆에서 버텨야 했다."
- "반품되지 않은 이름."
- "다음 섹터의 파일이 ... 손가락 아래 놓였다."

These are effective as research markers, but production S4 should vary closure:

- sometimes close on a physical object position;
- sometimes close on a practical instruction;
- sometimes close on an adult's conditional line;
- sometimes close on a clock or access limit.

Do not always close with a polished thesis sentence.
