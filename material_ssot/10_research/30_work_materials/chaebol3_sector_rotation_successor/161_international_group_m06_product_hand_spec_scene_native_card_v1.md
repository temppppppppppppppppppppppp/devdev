## 4. Writer-Visible Dispatch

Use only this stripped view for prose:

```text
Start at the quality table, not the pay window. The 40% notice exists in the background, but the object is a sample shoe in Jo Youngmi's hand.

Show sole adhesion, stitch line, ankle cloth rub, glue smell, defect tray, normal/repair/exclude piles. Jo resists because bad product kills the next order. Jang resists because cutting the attractive variant lowers output and buyer appeal. Han resists because passed-count release fragments payroll again.

Doyun must not design a new shoe. He cuts the flashy unsafe decoration-line variant and keeps the plain/basic line that survives hand pressure.

Payment: Jo writes touch-based spec lines, Jang and Han sign objections, repair pay releases only by 30-pair passed inspection increments, and the limited spec sheet exists under objection.

End with a sample box prepared for outside buyer/distributor desk. No designer genius, no worker cheer, no full payroll rescue.
```

## 5. Pass / Fail

Pass if:

- the product is handled, bent, pressed, rubbed, or sorted on page;
- Jo or quality actor resists for product liability;
- Doyun subtracts a risky attractive variant rather than inventing;
- passed-count payment condition appears;
- limited spec sheet is signed under objection;
- next gate moves outside to buyer/distributor desk.

Fail if:

- M06 repeats payroll negotiation;
- Doyun becomes a genius designer;
- quality actor passively agrees;
- product proof is only described, not touched;
- payment is full product success or brand praise;
- next gate stays inside factory quality table.
