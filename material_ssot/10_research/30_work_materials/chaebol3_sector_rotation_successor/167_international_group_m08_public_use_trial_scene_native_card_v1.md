## 4. Writer-Visible Dispatch

Use only this stripped view for prose:

```text
Start inside the school gym/equipment room. Do not start at buyer desk. Put the Haemun boxes under a bench or near an equipment rack. The teacher has an attendance/equipment sheet and does not want parent complaints.

Students must notice the Haemun name. Their reaction should be low-status, not praise. A coach/teacher controls the moment and refuses to use all 30 pairs.

Doyun must ask for less use: open 12, use 6, hold risky sizes, classify discomfort before it becomes parent complaint.

One student must report a real fit/heel/sole/lace discomfort on page. Teacher pulls the pair, same size is paused, and the complaint is written.

Payment: remaining unopened boxes are not returned. The Haemun name stays on a temporary equipment memo with active-use count, complaint count, and next-day recheck.

End with a path to internal budget/file access. No applause, no school adoption, no sales triumph.
```

## 5. Pass / Fail

Pass if:

- school gym/equipment room appears before explanation;
- teacher or coach protects students and parent-call risk;
- students notice the Haemun name without clean praise;
- only part of the trial opens;
- one fit/defect complaint is handled on page;
- remaining boxes are held, not returned;
- next gate moves to internal budget/file access.

Fail if:

- M08 repeats buyer ledger negotiation;
- public exposure becomes praise or adoption;
- no actual complaint happens;
- teacher/coach passively allows use;
- Doyun hides or minimizes the complaint;
- all 30 pairs are released;
- the next gate is another product/sales meeting instead of budget/file access.
