## 4. Writer-Visible Dispatch

Use only this stripped view for prose:

```text
Start at the outside buyer/distributor desk, not the factory. Put the sample box on the desk near the return ledger and phone. The front display is already filled by safer brands.

The buyer must protect his own risk before opening the box: who receives school calls, who handles returns, what happens if shipment is late, and whether the product can avoid the front shelf.

Doyun must ask for less than a victory. He should not ask for front display, praise, or a full order. He lowers the trial quantity and accepts visible conditions.

Payment: the buyer writes a conditional trial in his own ledger/form, the box is not returned, and it stays beside the phone/return ledger. The condition must include return cap, call handling, and delivery penalty or hold.

End by pointing to public-use trial: school gym, equipment room, teacher/coach handling, or a first shipment list. No sales triumph.
```

## 5. Pass / Fail

Pass if:

- the buyer desk, phone, return ledger, sample box, and front shelf appear on page;
- the buyer asks risk questions before curiosity or praise;
- Doyun lowers quantity or exposure;
- return/call/delivery conditions are written by the buyer side;
- the box remains in place but not on the front display;
- next gate moves to school/public-use trial.

Fail if:

- M07 repeats internal product/spec approval;
- buyer admires the product or trusts Doyun too quickly;
- front display or full order is granted;
- conditions are summarized but not written/handled on page;
- return/complaint risk is absent;
- the next gate is another buyer meeting instead of public use.
