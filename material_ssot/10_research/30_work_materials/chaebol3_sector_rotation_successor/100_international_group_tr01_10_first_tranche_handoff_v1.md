- disposal cover moved back;
- memo inserted into bank route;
- ink pad closes;

### Lock 2 - B3/B4 may compress into one EP003 motion

The canary proved that telex, buyer reply, Busan payroll, and fax can work as one escalating sequence.

Do not force an artificial episode split if the prose works better as:

`telex desk friction -> buyer cost reply -> hostile Busan numbers -> fax clipped to bank line`.

### Lock 3 - B5 begins after the crisis note has already opened

- "박윤재 writes 문도윤's name" as if it is new;
- "the crisis cell begins" as the only payment.

### Lock 4 - B6-B10 must escape paperwork gravity

Mandatory reward-token rotation:

- B6: pay window / factory floor / shipment line;
- B7: defect tray / limited spec sheet;
- B8: sample box / return-condition sheet;
- B9: equipment bag / receipt / replacement slip;

## 4. First-Tranche Handoff Units

Each unit below is an operator handoff. For S4, expose only the compact dispatch and the "must move / must resist / must not" lines.

### H01. EP001 / B1-B2: Stamp Does Not Land

Reader payment:

- 문도윤 is still only `문준하 아들`;
- but the stamp route changes because of his line.

Compact dispatch:

```text
Start in the export-finance corridor outside the creditor-bank meeting. 문도윤 wakes in his young body holding the emergency folder. Use folder edge, ink smell, date stamp, phone bell, and `문준하 아들` as the first pressure. The disposal cover hides L/C confirmation, shipping deadline, and Busan payroll memo. He writes one short 72-hour verification hold line. The disposal cover moves back, the ink pad closes, the approval sheet turns sideways, and 김태완 asks who wrote the line. No future-history lecture. No adult praise.
```

Must move:

- disposal cover;
- ink pad;
- approval sheet;
- bank question.

Must resist:

- 박윤재 protects meeting accident risk;
- 문성필 protects clean-sale momentum;
- 김태완 protects bank exposure.

Must not:

- grant 72 hours as a win yet;
- explain International Group history;
- turn this into a genius declaration.

### H02. EP002 / B2-B3: Watched Telex Chair

Reader payment:

- 문도윤 gets one watched chair and a failure condition, not recognition.

Compact dispatch:

```text
Start from 김태완 and 문태섭 distrusting the memo. 문도윤 asks for one outgoing telex and 72 hours only. He accepts a failure condition: if buyer continuity and Busan payroll exposure do not prove out, he loses corridor, folder, sales-office, and document access. 김태완 leaves the bank approval line blank. 박윤재 writes the telex-room chair and supervision condition. 문태섭 makes the hold humiliating, not warm.
```

Must move:

- blank approval line;
- pen note;
- watched chair;
- access-loss condition.

Must resist:

- 문태섭 protects founder authority;
- 김태완 protects audit trail;
- 문성필 protects the sale route.

Must not:

- make the hold praise;
- soften 문태섭;
- let the bank believe him for free.
