## 1. Why This Exists

The Firefly-side M01-M02 dryrun proved that S4 can write a more human-like commercial opening when the material hands it:

- a live object that could move;
- a visible status wound;
- hidden loss surfaces;
- a smaller-than-victory ask;
- a blank or withheld surface;
- a resistant adult hand;
- watched access;
- a written failure condition.

This file applies that card back to the material side before any wider B3-B10, B11-B20, BI, or TR70 expansion.

## 6. Pass / Fail For This Application

Pass only if the next prose canary shows:

- body/status pressure before history;
- at least one adult hand still blocks or controls the object;
- two payments are physical and limited;
- no adult praise;
- the next scene surface changes from meeting room to telex/export desk, then toward Busan/buyer evidence.

Fail if:

- the card turns into completed YAML with dead prose;
- it repeats stamp/document payment without opening the telex chair;
- Doyun speaks like a consultant for more than one breath;
- the ending says he gained authority instead of showing watched access and written failure condition;
- the next gate loops back into another abstract meeting.
