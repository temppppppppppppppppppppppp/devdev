## 2. Pass 1: S4 Usability

Question:

Can S4 write from this without receiving the whole research stack?

Answer: yes, with watch.

Strong points:

- It gives rooms before concepts: corridor, meeting table, telex desk.
- It gives touchable objects: folder, stamp pad, approval line, telex chair, supervision note.
- It gives rational resistance instead of stupid blocking.
- It pays with limited access, not full authority.

Watch:

- The writer-visible notes are still slightly dense.
- If copied whole into a prompt without taste, S4 may still over-explain the commercial mechanism.
- The operator should pass the writer-visible note plus the S4 context fill, then explicitly say: do not explain the entire mechanism before the object moves.

## 3. Pass 2: Anti-AI-Slop Read

Primary AI-authorship risk:

- every beat is too neatly named;
- adults behave like they exist to satisfy the material contract;
- Doyun's line becomes a perfect clever sentence instead of a tense small intervention.

Required patch discipline in any prose dryrun:

- put a small delay or awkwardness before the line works;
- make the protocol aide physically keep the folder away from Doyun;
- let the bank officer protect himself before curiosity appears;
- let grandfather's refusal remain emotionally cold;
- keep one objection visible at the end.

Reject prose if it says:

- "This changed the authority structure";
- "The room finally understood";
- "The reward was access";
- "Doyun proved his competence";
- "The next gate opened."

Those are audit summaries, not manuscript sentences.

## 4. Pass 3: Pacing And Reader Payment

Does M01-M02 resemble the successful-manuscript lesson?

Partial yes.

The opening pays fast enough if EP001 ends on the stamp not landing and EP002 ends on the watched telex chair. That gives a real 100-won receipt before broader exposition.

Risk:

- If EP001 spends too long on return shock or group history, the stamp payment arrives late.
- If EP002 debates the whole restructuring logic, the telex chair arrives late.
- If both episodes stay in the same meeting-room tone, the old BI/TR pacing problem returns.

Mitigation:

- EP001 should be corridor/folder/stamp.
- EP002 should move from table/approval line to actual telex-room chair.
- The location shift is not cosmetic; it is the proof that material has escaped same-room drag.

## 6. Remaining Weaknesses

1. Paper/stamp/line gravity is still high.
   - This is acceptable for B1-B2, but B3-B6 must move into phone, telex machine, fax, payroll window, factory floor, and product hand.

2. Personal ache is present but thin.
   - The father-dead/direct-blood wound should appear through how adults use Doyun, not through a backstory paragraph.

3. Doyun's one line can become too polished.
   - In prose, it should feel like a risky short mark under pressure, not a perfect boardroom diagnosis.

4. The file is still a material artifact, not a final S4 prompt.
   - A future handoff should strip sections 0-3 and 6-8 unless the operator is doing Director review.
