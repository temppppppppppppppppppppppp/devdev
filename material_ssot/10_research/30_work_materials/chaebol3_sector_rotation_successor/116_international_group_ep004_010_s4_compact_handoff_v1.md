## 1. Operator Intent

The first ten episode material should teach S4 one thing:

Mun Doyun wins by asking for a smaller permission that can survive inside the room.

The scene does not pay him with praise. It pays him by leaving an object where it could have been removed:

- a task sheet stays open;
- a pay notice stays on the board;
- a plain sample remains in the box;
- a sample box stays beside the buyer's phone;
- a school memo keeps the Haemun name below the equipment list;
- a finance card gives one hour with the import-material file.

Every room must have an adult who resists for a practical reason: audit, payroll face, factory blame, quality credibility, return calls, student complaints, budget precedent, or old family control.

## 2. Writer-Facing Payload

### EP004 - First Task, Not Authority

Open after the crisis note is already on the table.

Mun Doyun is not given decision power. Mun Seongpil narrows the job to document preparation. The bank-side man wants fields that will not embarrass him under audit. The room has a waiting-room phone, three document bundles, report times, and a task sheet with Doyun's name inside the line.

Doyun does not argue that he knows the future. He asks for the phone route and file route under supervision. He accepts the small job because it can be executed today.

End with the task sheet still open, the phone number copied, and the next report time fixed.

Avoid:

- chairman suddenly trusting him;
- abstract family recognition;
- a clean victory speech;
- future-knowledge explanation before the document work moves.

### EP005 - Busan Pay Window

Move to Busan.

Show empty pay envelopes, workers watching the pay window, a factory board, one shipment line that may die today, and a defect tray no one wants to own.

The local manager protects Busan's face. The factory-side man protects himself from blame. Nobody wants an unknown heir's name on a public notice.

Doyun removes his own name from the posted pay notice. He separates the first pay schedule from the product-quality problem. The notice only promises 40 percent first, not a rescue.

End with the 40 percent notice on the board, one shipment line still alive, and the defect tray becoming the next condition.

Avoid:

- making workers grateful too fast;
- using the heir name as a magic key;
- solving wages and product quality in one gesture;
- letting the factory opposition vanish.

### EP006 - Product In Hand

Keep the camera on hands and shoes.

Use a quality table, sample shoes, a defect tray, a limited spec sheet, and passed-count payment. The quality person protects credibility. The factory man wants quantity because lost quantity becomes his blame.

Doyun cuts the shiny line that will return. He keeps the plain line that survives the hand. He does not ask people to believe him. He lets the failed sample, the pass count, and the payment rule do the work.

End with the plain line still in the box, the shiny line cut, and money tied to passed samples.

Avoid:

- product genius monologue;
- flashy design win;
- perfect quality result;
- a clean factory cheer.

### EP007 - Buyer Desk

Put the sample box on a distributor or school-supply buyer's desk.

The buyer sees the Haemun mark as risk. He asks about return rate, parent calls, delivery penalty, and shelf space before he opens the box. The desk has a phone, return ledger, pencil marks, and other products already claiming the front.

Doyun lowers exposure. A bigger ask becomes 30 pairs under the buyer's terms. He offers a return cap and support windows.

End with the sample box not returned. It remains beside the buyer's phone and return ledger, not on the front shelf.

Avoid:

- buyer admiring the product;
- front-display victory;
- big order;
- clean comeback language.

### EP008-009 - School Gym

Put the boxes into a school gym and equipment room.

The teacher protects students, complaint risk, and her own name on school paper. She does not want a promotion event. She limits opened pairs and watches for discomfort.

One heel-rub complaint appears on page. Doyun does not hide it. He cuts opened quantity to 12, separates discomfort from product failure, and keeps the remaining boxes from being returned.

End with the Haemun name written below the equipment list, no photo taken, one complaint recorded, and the remaining boxes still in the equipment room.

Avoid:

- clean student applause;
- promo-photo smell;
- hiding the complaint;
- making public use look like an advertisement.

### EP009-010 - Finance Room And File Hour

Move to a small finance meeting room, not the original crisis room.

Bring the distributor paper, school memo, pay notice, spec sheet, and defect note. Mun Seongpil attacks the result as too small: six gym uses, one heel-rub recall, no brand restoration. The finance person protects budget precedent and file-room hierarchy.

Doyun does not call it a comeback. He asks for less: 100,000 won, one hour, and one import-material file. The earlier defect makes the file request practical rather than grand.

End with the finance card giving exactly one hour, the import-material file on the desk, and the clock already running.

Avoid:

- budget victory;
- family approval scene;
- treating school proof as enough;
- opening the next sector before the file is physically in hand.

## 4. S4 Safety Notes

Keep each win smaller than Doyun wants.

Keep one objection visible in the paper, memo, ledger, or person's behavior.

Do not explain the mechanism after every scene. After the first clear line, let hands, phones, boards, boxes, clocks, and doors carry the pressure.

Vary the final beat. Do not always end with a polished thesis sentence. Sometimes end on the object staying put, sometimes on an instruction, sometimes on a clock, sometimes on an adult's conditional line.
