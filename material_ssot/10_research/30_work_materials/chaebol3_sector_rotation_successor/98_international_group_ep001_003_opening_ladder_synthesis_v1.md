## 3. What We Learned From The Canaries

### 3.1 The useful material unit is not "권한 이동"

The useful unit is:

`which paper/object/seat/line moved, who moved it, and what cost made them move it`.

Examples:

- 인주 뚜껑 closes instead of abstract "procedure changed";
- 결재란 turns sideways instead of "bank became interested";
- 텔렉스실 의자 opens instead of "access granted";
- 부산 급여표 is clipped beside the pending approval line instead of "outside evidence arrived";
- 위기 셀 노트 begins under supervision instead of "status reframe."

### 3.2 The protagonist must not be believed too early

The best pressure in EP001-EP003 comes from adults moving while still doubting him.

- 박윤재 moves because of 결재 사고 risk.
- 김태완 moves because an audit trail is safer than deleting a weird memo.
- 문태섭 grants time as punishment/condition, not affection.
- 오동식 cooperates because the bank memo makes the outgoing text his liability.
- 한기철 answers because numbers should remain, not because he trusts Seoul.

This should become a reusable Firefly material rule:

`No trust payment before object payment.`

### 3.3 The opening succeeds because it rotates rooms before the concept gets stale

The same problem continues, but the surface rotates:

- EP001: corridor and folder;
- EP002: bank/founder room and approval line;
- EP003: telex room, export desk, phone, fax, payroll sheet.

This answers the user's earlier fear about old BI/TR pacing: "10화 동안 같은 장소 같은 주제 같은 소재" can be prevented only if the material locks surface rotation, not just event progression.

### 3.4 The emotional reward is not huge, but it is commercially legible

The first three episodes do not need:

- profit;
- public praise;
- successor claim;
- grandfather apology;
- famous-person gate.

They need the reader to feel:

`방금까지 폴더 심부름꾼이던 애 때문에 어른들이 자기 책임을 피하려고 절차를 바꿨다.`

That is a valid first-three-episode reward for this genre line.
