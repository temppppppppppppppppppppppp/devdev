## Pass 1: Historical / Business Plausibility Audit

### Verdict

`PASS_WITH_CORRECTION_REQUIRED`

### What Works

- The International Group / Kukje reference is a strong premise engine because it gives the opening inherited shame, creditor pressure, political-bank coercion, Busan/local cost, and a public restoration arc.
- The move from abstract "collapse" to `export LC + shipping manifest + supplier payroll + bank telex` is the correct direction.
- The grandfather's mistake is now dramatizable: he sacrifices a "small" affiliate without seeing the guarantee/export/payroll chain.

### Failure Risks

#### Risk 1: One-document miracle

If Phase0 or S4 writes this as "the boy found one LC and saved the group," it becomes fake-business fantasy.

Correction:

- The LC must buy only `72 hours`, not rescue.
- The first proof should show `immediate sale is more expensive than delay`, not "the protagonist has the correct answer."
- The group remains in danger after EP003.

Required line for Phase0:

`The LC is not the solution. It is the first wedge that makes the bank officer pause before stamping the breakup route.`

#### Risk 2: Bank/legal mechanics are still under-specified

`bank line freeze telex`, `creditor-bank memo`, `affiliate guarantee chain`, and `72-hour hold` are good objects, but the mechanism is not yet locked.

Missing answers:

- Who legally controls the hold: bank officer, creditor committee, chairman, or government-facing intermediary?
- What exactly is being held: affiliate sale, check dishonor, shipping release, loan acceleration, or asset transfer?
- What is the bank officer's personal risk if he accepts the hold?

Correction:

- Before final Phase0, choose one primary mechanism.
- Recommendation: `hold the emergency sale / breakup action for 72 hours while confirming foreign buyer continuity and supplier-payroll exposure`.
- Avoid a courtroom/legal-injunction opening unless the work wants a legal thriller tone.

#### Risk 3: Grandfather's mistake may over-villainize him

If grandfather is written as foolish or corrupt, the story loses restoration dignity.

Correction:

- He should be competent in the old export-builder mode but blind to paper-chain / bank-political coercion mechanics.
- His first concession must be tactical, not emotional surrender.
- He should attach a harsh condition to protect face and authority.

Good concession:

- "You get the telex room until midnight. If the buyer does not answer, you never touch a group folder again."

Bad concession:

- "I was wrong. You are the heir."

## Pass 2: Opening / Reader-Seat / S4 Scene-Native Audit

### Verdict

`PASS_WITH_WATCH`

### What Works

- EP001 now has a concrete job frame: fatherless direct-line grandson carrying an emergency export/finance folder.
- The first adult movement is visible: secretary changes the folder route.
- The first reward is access, not money: one chair outside bank meeting, telex phone access, memo in folder.
- The seed explicitly avoids Jobs/Musk/Son in the opening, which is correct.

### Failure Risks

#### Risk 1: First 300-500 characters may become jargon

If the opening begins with LC, guarantees, creditor committee, and telex explanation, it will feel like a report.

Correction:

First 300-500 characters should be:

- body rupture;
- fatherless status;
- object in hand;
- one visible adult dismissal;
- one sensory marker of 1985.

Then LC mechanics can enter through action.

Required opening pressure formula:

`몸/날짜 불일치 -> 폴더를 든 손 -> "문준하 아들" 취급 -> 급한 도장/회의 -> 밑장에 낀 LC 발견`

#### Risk 2: 19-year-old expertise is not yet earned

A 19-year-old seeing a bank/export trap is satisfying only if the text gives him a believable tool.

Correction:

Use old-life scar:

- In the failed future, he spent years reading liquidation records, lawsuit exhibits, creditor memos, and auction notices after the group collapse.
- He does not know business because he is a genius student.
- He recognizes the killing pattern because he already lived through the postmortem documents.

Do not make him broadly omniscient in EP001.

Allowed:

- "This LC number had been printed on the first lawsuit exhibit I ever memorized."

Forbidden:

- "He knew the global economy for the next forty years."

#### Risk 3: Secretary route-change may be too quiet as first reward

File movement is good, but if too subtle, the reader may not feel payment.

Correction:

The route-change must create a visible social change:

- the secretary stops calling him by child/family nickname and uses surname/title;
- the folder leaves the family-copy pile and goes into the bank-meeting leather case;
- he is told to wait by the telex phone, not in the hallway;
- one uncle notices and reacts.

Minimum EP001 receipt:

`memo moved + chair assigned + phone access + named adult asks who wrote it`

## Pass 3: Pipeline / Material-Readiness / Canary Audit

- S4's ability to avoid report prose.

It cannot test:

- only EP001 first scene;

- no global future-history lecture.

#### Risk 2: Current work_guard is still 2008/NDF-specific

The work_guard draft passed WG-V1, but it was built around the old 2008 Taerim/NDF engine.

Correction:

Do not publish/freeze current work_guard for the International Group baseline.

Required Arc 02 receipt:

`factory schedule funded + worker/payroll trust moves + conditional retail/athlete gate + public name visible + internal budget for next technology/trading door`
