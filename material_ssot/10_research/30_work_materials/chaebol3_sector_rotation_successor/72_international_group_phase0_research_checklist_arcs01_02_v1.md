# International-Group Phase0 Research Checklist: Arcs 01-02

Date: 2026-05-17
Status: pre-Phase0 checklist, first two arcs only
Work ID: `chaebol3_sector_rotation_successor`

## 0. Purpose

This checklist narrows the post-canary research into the first two macro arcs.

Do not research the whole world-top rise first.

First prove that the 1984-1988 opening can produce scenes with:

- human pressure;
- resistant adults;
- paper/object movement;
- narrow protagonist action;
- visible reward token;
- next gate.

If this does not work, later Apple/Musk/Son gates will become empty fan-service.

## 1. Arc 01: Save The Name, 1984-1986

### 1.1 Required Historical Pressure

Need enough source-backed substrate to fictionalize:

- International/Kukje-style group dissolution/disbandment pressure;
- creditor-bank dependency;
- political/financial authority pressure;
- export receivables and letters of credit;
- Busan supplier/port damage;
- affiliate guarantee chain;
- emergency asset sale route;
- grandfather's fatal misread.

Source anchors already checked:

- `https://www.upi.com/Archives/1993/07/29/Disbandment-of-Kukje-Group-declared-unconstitutional/4783743918400/`
- `https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/lifestyle/20090329/ex-kukje-chairman-dies`
- `https://www.bizhankook.com/bk/article/20823`
- `https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-03-28-fi-29276-story.html`

Need deeper pass:

- affiliate list and fictional substitution map;
- how state-controlled creditor pressure could be shown without making a legal documentary;
- which object first moves: credit-line telex, LC confirmation, shipping manifest, supplier payroll ledger, injunction draft, or board memo;
- what the grandfather did wrong in a scene, not an essay.

### 1.2 First Arc Scene Surfaces

Candidate surfaces:

- main creditor bank corridor;
- group chairman's office;
- export department after-hours desk;
- Busan supplier association room;
- port customs/shipping office;
- emergency asset-sale meeting;
- family dining room only if a paper/object enters and changes hands.

Avoid:

- long political exposition;
- pure grandfather blame monologue;
- grandson proving everything by future-knowledge speech;
- collapse montage with no current-room action.

### 1.3 Required Resistant Actors

Need at least four:

- grandfather: proud, politically misreading, unwilling to let a grandson touch crisis files;
- main bank officer: afraid of superior order and loan exposure;
- family uncle/cousin: wants to sell one affiliate and secure personal survival;
- export buyer or shipping counterparty: wants goods delivered, not family drama;
- Busan supplier representative: angry because payment freeze kills payroll;
- secretary/legal officer: protects procedure, not the protagonist.

Each blocker must be rational.

Weak blocker:

- "He dislikes the protagonist."

Useful blocker:

- "If he accepts the protagonist's memo, he owns the risk if the bank order fails."

### 1.4 Protagonist Levers

The protagonist must not solve 1985 Korea with one genius speech.

Allowed narrow levers:

- find one LC that can still be confirmed by foreign buyer;
- show that one affiliate sale triggers larger guarantee default;
- identify a shipping document that proves delivery risk to a foreign counterparty;
- ask for a 72-hour hold instead of full rescue;
- force grandfather to let him run a crisis cell under conditions;
- move one document from family shame into creditor-bank cost.

### 1.5 Arc 01 Reward Tokens

Minimum rewards before moving to Arc 02:

- one bank/creditor deadline is delayed;
- one foreign buyer or port actor becomes an external witness;
- one family adult stops treating the protagonist as decorative;
- one crisis-cell authority token is issued;
- one saved affiliate becomes the bridge to consumer-brand revival.

## 2. Arc 02: Make The Name Visible, 1986-1988

### 2.1 Required Historical Pressure

Need enough substrate to fictionalize:

- Prospecs-like footwear/sportswear brand;
- Korean export footwear and domestic sportswear timing;
- Seoul Olympics visibility;
- retail distribution and athlete/association sponsorship;
- factory labor/payment pressure after group survival;
- brand legitimacy after a public collapse scare.

Source anchors already checked:

- `https://www.lsnetworks.com/images/ir/2011%20AR_Eng.pdf`
- `https://www.bizhankook.com/bk/article/20823`
- `https://www.insight.co.kr/news/196185`

Need deeper pass:

- what product line is fictionally safe to use;
- what Olympics-adjacent gate is plausible without claiming official sponsorship too early;
- what factory/retail surface shows money and pride at the same time;
- how the brand revival opens trading/licensing or semiconductor-supplier doors.

### 2.2 Second Arc Scene Surfaces

Candidate surfaces:

- shoe factory line with defect/late-payment pressure;
- athlete endorsement negotiation;
- sports association office;
- department store buyer meeting;
- TV ad slot or magazine shoot;
- export buyer showroom;
- logistics schedule for peak demand;
- hotel/civic reception where the restored name becomes visible.

Avoid:

- "we make a popular brand" summary;
- montage of sales numbers only;
- protagonist designing shoes like a fashion genius without industrial reason;
- Olympics name-dropping without a contract, distribution, or production gate.

### 2.3 Required Resistant Actors

Need at least four:

- factory manager: fears overpromising after near-collapse;
- labor/payroll representative: does not trust owner-family promises;
- athlete/agent: does not want a damaged group name;
- distributor/buyer: wants proof of supply, returns, and ad support;
- family old guard: thinks consumer brand is beneath heavy-industry restoration;
- media/ad buyer: fears controversy around the group.

### 2.4 Protagonist Levers

Allowed narrow levers:

- secure one supply payment schedule that keeps the factory running;
- convert saved export buyer trust into domestic quality proof;
- negotiate athlete endorsement with downside protection;
- use Olympics traffic without claiming impossible official control;
- tie brand visibility to restored group credit;
- make grandfather publicly accept a consumer-facing win.

### 2.5 Arc 02 Reward Tokens

Minimum rewards before moving to technology spine:

- production schedule is funded and witnessed;
- one athlete/distributor/retail gate signs conditionally;
- the group name appears in a public context without shame;
- factory/labor trust improves through payment, not speech;
- consumer cash flow gives the protagonist a legitimate internal budget;
- the next gate opens: trading/licensing or semiconductor supplier investment.

## 3. Phase0 Stopline

Before full Phase0, answer these:

1. What exact document/object opens episode 1 after regression?
2. Which adult first changes behavior because of protagonist action?
3. What is the first reward token inside 1-2 episodes?
4. What does grandfather concede, and what condition does he attach?
5. Which saved affiliate bridges from survival to sportswear/consumer brand?
6. What proves Arc 02 is not just sales montage?
7. What next gate logically follows from the consumer-brand win?

If these seven questions are unanswered, do not write TR 1-10.
