## 2. Human-Likeness / Style Audit

### What Works

The scene is strongest where people avoid saying the mechanism:

- 신동규 taps the glass instead of explaining budget precedent.
- 문성필 attacks the smallness of the result.
- 도윤 admits "권리 없습니다."
- The teacher memo has no praise, which makes it usable.
- The green file-room card does the reward work.

This is materially better than old broad BI/TR because the writer is not trying to fill an abstract arc. The room itself gives pressure.

### Patch Recommended

The following lines are a little too thesis-clean:

- `칭찬은 없었다. 추천도 없었다. 그래서 버틸 수 있는 종이였다.`
- `감시는 허락의 다른 이름이었다.`

They are not fatal, but they sound like the writer naming the mechanism. Replace at least one with physical behavior.

Suggested patch direction:

- Instead of saying the paper is strong because it lacks praise, show 신동규 not crossing it out.
- Instead of "surveillance is permission," show the file staying open while 신동규 remains standing.

### Watch Item

The dialogue is mostly functional, but some exchanges are very crisp. In later smoke tests, add one practical interruption:

- a staff member asking whether the file can leave the shelf;
- a phone ringing from another desk;
- 문성필 misreading the request as budget approval;
- 신동규 correcting the exact accounting category.

The point is not messiness for its own sake. The page needs office friction so it does not feel like a proof ladder conversation.
