## 3. Arc 01 Contract

Arc 01 promise:

`The boy treated as a folder courier becomes the person the bank and grandfather need in order not to make a more expensive mistake.`

Arc 01 must pay these receipts in order:

1. Procedure movement: folder/stamp/telex access changes because of 문도윤.
2. Conditional time: 72 hours open under failure conditions.
3. External witness: buyer/payroll/shipping proof leaves the chairman-room.
4. Authority token: supervised crisis cell or saved-affiliate task opens.
5. Bridge: the saved affiliate becomes a factory/product/public-name surface, not abstract group rescue.

Hard boundaries:

- LC buys time only.
- Adults move because their risk changed, not because they trust 문도윤.
- No Jobs/Musk/Son/Apple/SoftBank/Tesla/AI/global ladder in Arc 01.
- No group rescue, no grandfather surrender, no successor declaration.
- No endless chairman-room argument.
- No raw donor prose, donor dialogue, donor order, or donor object chain.

## 5. Scene Units

### A01-S01. Wrong Body, Right Folder

Function:

- regression ignition plus job frame;
- reader attachment before business explanation;
- first visible price: 문도윤 has blood legitimacy but no operating authority.

Visible surface:

- export/finance corridor outside an emergency bank meeting;
- open stamp pad;
- `수출금융 긴급철`;
- disposal folder cover;
- rotary phone, rubber/ink smell, date stamp, wrist/skin mismatch.

Live transaction:

- `해문산업 긴급 처분안 결재` is about to enter the bank meeting route.

Protagonist pressure:

- 문도윤 is the dead heir's son and alive only as a corridor errand body;
- adults call him `문준하 아들`;
- if he hands over the folder untouched, his old-life collapse begins again.

Proof object/action:

- he notices a buried `L/C 확인 요청`, shipping deadline, and Busan payroll note under the disposal sheet;
- he writes only a short hold line, not a future-history explanation.

Resistant witness and reason:

- 박윤재 protects procedure and meeting liability;
- 문성필 wants clean disposal momentum;
- both are rational because a nineteen-year-old handling a crisis folder is a liability.

Actor movement before receipt:

- 박윤재 stops taking the folder straight to the stamp line;
- the disposal cover moves behind the memo;
- the stamp pad stays open but unused for one beat.

Paid receipt:

- 문도윤 is no longer just carrying the folder; his line is inside the meeting route.

Next gate:

- 김태완 must decide whether the memo changes the bank's own risk.

S4 compact handoff:

- 문도윤 wakes into the corridor with the wrong wrist and the right crisis folder.
- Adults tell `문준하 아들` not to touch contents.
- He sees `L/C 확인 요청`, a shipping deadline, and Busan payroll pressure under the disposal sheet.
- He writes a short 72-hour hold line.
- The disposal cover is pushed back; his memo goes behind the bank-meeting first page.

Forbidden flattenings:

- do not open with International Group history;
- do not let 문도윤 explain 2030s memory;
- do not make 박윤재 impressed;
- do not solve anything yet.

### A01-S02. The Hold Is Not Trust

Function:

- first proof becomes conditional time, not recognition.

Visible surface:

- secretary desk and creditor-bank meeting threshold;
- stopped stamp line;
- 김태완's approval line;
- telex request form;
- chair placed just inside the telex room.

Live transaction:

- approve immediate disposal/credit-freeze route or hold it for verification.

Protagonist pressure:

- 문태섭 is angry that the child touched a sealed crisis folder;
- 문성필 tries to remove 문도윤 from the room;
- if the hold fails, 문도윤 loses corridor/folder access.

Proof object/action:

- 문도윤 asks for one outgoing telex and 72 hours;
- condition: if buyer continuity is false, disposal proceeds;
- if buyer continuity is true, immediate disposal makes the bank own a larger export/payroll failure.

Resistant witness and reason:

- 김태완 resists because the bank cannot freeze a group based on a boy's memo;
- 문태섭 resists because a founder cannot let a corridor child steer crisis procedure;
- 문성필 resists because sale momentum protects his line.

Actor movement before receipt:

- 김태완 taps the LC number on the approval line instead of stamping;
- 박윤재 closes the ink pad;
- a telex-room chair is assigned under observation.

Paid receipt:

- 72-hour hold opens, but only as a verification procedure with a failure condition.

Next gate:

- buyer and Busan answers must come back before the hold expires.

S4 compact handoff:

- 김태완 does not praise 문도윤.
- He asks who wrote the LC line because the approval line now exposes the bank.
- 문태섭 allows one telex only under 박윤재's note.
- 문도윤 gets a chair, not a title.

Forbidden flattenings:

- no grandfather surrender;
- no "the bank believed him";
- no legal lecture;
- no 72-hour hold without failure penalty.

### A01-S03. Telex Room: Proof Leaves The Family Room

Function:

- move the proof surface out of family argument and into an external confirmation channel.

Visible surface:

- narrow telex room;
- export desk;
- carbon copy paper;
- buyer code;
- shipping schedule board.

Live transaction:

- whether the foreign buyer still wants shipment from the operating factory, not a broken asset transfer.

Protagonist pressure:

- every delayed minute gives 문성필 a reason to call the hold damage;
- 문도윤 must stay precise because any overclaim ends his access.

Proof object/action:

- he composes a confirmation request using current LC/shipping references;
- he asks for continuity of order, shipment condition, cancellation cost, and response deadline.

Resistant witness and reason:

- export staff dislike taking instructions from a grandson;
- 박윤재 watches for procedural contamination;
- 김태완 wants a clean answer he can defend upward.

Actor movement before receipt:

- export staff pull the shipping copy;
- the telex operator sends the request ahead of a routine stack;
- 김태완 keeps the approval line unsigned while waiting.

Paid receipt:

- external reply begins to form: the buyer cares about shipment continuity and delay cost, not family pride.

Next gate:

- Busan payroll and line continuity must prove the cost is human and immediate.

S4 compact handoff:

- 문도윤 sits at the telex room chair under adult eyes.
- He does not ask the buyer to save the group.
- He asks what happens if the shipment stops today.
- The telex operator moves his request above the routine pile.

Forbidden flattenings:

- no instant clean reply;
- no buyer praise;
- no "international trade lecture";
- no scene that stays only in Seoul offices.

### A01-S04. Busan Payroll: The Price Gets A Face

Function:

- turn bank/export risk into human cost and local pressure.

Visible surface:

- Busan factory payroll office or phone/fax surface connected to it;
- wage ledger;
- supplier delivery slip;
- loading schedule;
- foreman's waiting body, not a speech.

Live transaction:

- if disposal/freezing proceeds, wages and supplier payment fail before the buyer reply can matter.

Protagonist pressure:

- 문도윤 must prove he is not playing a boardroom game with workers' money;
- this is where future memory becomes present accountability.

Proof object/action:

- he asks 한기철 for exact payroll day, unpaid amount, shipment quantity, and stoppage trigger;
- he ties those numbers back to the bank's exposure window.

Resistant witness and reason:

- 한기철 distrusts Seoul family members because they arrive after the factory is already bleeding;
- Busan staff have no reason to protect 문도윤's pride.

Actor movement before receipt:

- 한기철 sends or reads the payroll ledger line;
- a loading schedule is held rather than canceled;
- the bank note gains a local-cost attachment.

Paid receipt:

- immediate disposal now has a visible price: unpaid people, stopped shipment, and bank-owned blame.

Next gate:

- 문태섭 must decide whether to open a supervised crisis cell.

S4 compact handoff:

- The Busan voice is tired and hostile.
- It gives a wage date, not comfort.
- 문도윤 hears the line workers as the cost of the stamp.
- 김태완 now has a payroll attachment he cannot ignore.

Forbidden flattenings:

- no sentimental worker monologue;
- no union/politics lecture;
- no instant loyalty from Busan;
- no "payroll" label without amount/date/consequence.

### A01-S05. Crisis Cell: Authority Without Recognition

Function:

- convert proof into a narrow authority token.

Visible surface:

- chairman room with legal/secretary/bank notes on the same table;
- disposal approval line still unstamped;
- telex reply;
- Busan payroll attachment;
- crisis-cell notebook.

Live transaction:

- whether the first affiliate is sold immediately or held under an emergency operating cell.

Protagonist pressure:

- 문도윤 cannot demand inheritance;
- he must accept a harsh condition to keep access.

Proof object/action:

- he aligns buyer continuity, payroll risk, and bank exposure into one hold condition;
- he proposes what gets checked in 72 hours, who signs, who reports, and what fails if he is wrong.

Resistant witness and reason:

- 문태섭 resists because allowing a grandson into crisis procedure weakens founder authority;
- 김태완 resists because the bank still needs defensible paperwork;
- 문성필 resists because every hour weakens his clean-sale path.

Actor movement before receipt:

- 박윤재 writes 문도윤's name into the crisis-cell note as observer/task owner;
- 김태완 marks the bank line as pending verification;
- 문태섭 grants no title but assigns one task: keep the affiliate alive long enough to verify.

Paid receipt:

- `문준하 아들` becomes a supervised 72-hour responsible actor.

Next gate:

- turn the held affiliate into a payment schedule and product surface.

S4 compact handoff:

- 문태섭 does not soften.
- He gives a condition, a deadline, and a threat.
- 문도윤 receives no title, only one line in a crisis-cell notebook.
- 문성필 loses the clean stamp.

Forbidden flattenings:

- no emotional apology;
- no successor hint;
- no "everyone stared in admiration";
- no crisis cell without named task and failure condition.

### A01-S06. Payroll-To-Product Bridge

Function:

- optional Arc 01 tail or Arc 02 bridge;
- keep the saved affiliate from becoming abstract.

Visible surface:

- Busan factory floor;
- pay window;
- sample shoe/sportswear line;
- defect tray;
- shipment carton.

Live transaction:

- can the held affiliate produce one credible shipment and keep the line from walking out?

Protagonist pressure:

- if money does not reach people and production, the 72-hour hold was just paperwork.

Proof object/action:

- 문도윤 uses the crisis cell to open a limited payment route or escrow schedule;
- he does not promise a full rescue;
- he asks which order can ship without hiding defects.

Resistant witness and reason:

- factory manager and payroll staff distrust Seoul promises;
- workers will not stay for speeches.

Actor movement before receipt:

- one pay schedule is posted or confirmed;
- one shipment line is not dismantled;
- a defect/quality lead gives a limited production condition.

Paid receipt:

- the saved affiliate becomes an actual line, payroll, and product gate.

Next gate:

- distributor shelf or school supply trial order in Arc 02.

S4 compact handoff:

- The first public-name recovery begins with a wage window and a carton, not a slogan.
- The factory allows one schedule under condition.
- 문도윤 sees the thing the group name will be printed on.

Forbidden flattenings:

- no montage of sales success;
- no sudden worker loyalty;
- no brand campaign yet;
- no public recognition before payment/quality condition.
