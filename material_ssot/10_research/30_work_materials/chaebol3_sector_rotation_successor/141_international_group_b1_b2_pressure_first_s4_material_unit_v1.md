## 1. Purpose

The prior material proved that the first opening ladder can work, but it still risks becoming too clean, too paper-heavy, or too explanatory when handed to S4.

This unit re-cuts B1-B2 into a small scene-native packet:

- first show room/status/body pressure;
- then use objects and resisting adults;
- then let the protagonist ask for less than victory;
- then pay with object/access movement, not abstract authority.

The target is not "Doyun is smart."

The target is:

`Adults still doubt Doyun, but their own risk forces them to stop one procedure and open one supervised access route.`

## 3. B1-B2 Macro Truth

Return window:

- `1984 Q4 ~ 1985 Q1`.
- The group is near an International Group-like collapse.
- Grandfather made the fatal group-level judgment.
- Doyun's father died years ago in an accident.
- Doyun is direct blood, but inside the room he is treated as a disposable young family body carrying a folder.

Opening promise:

- The reader should not receive a market lecture first.
- The reader should see a young body, wrong time, folder pressure, adult dismissal, stamp risk, and one line that adults cannot safely ignore.

Reward shape:

`stamp does not land -> watched telex chair opens`

Not:

`group is saved -> adults admire him -> successor aura begins`

## 4. Compact Material Unit M01: B1 / EP001

Unit ID:

- `M01 / B1 / EP001 / stamp-does-not-land`

Room surface:

- Export-finance corridor just outside the creditor-bank meeting.
- The meeting room door is not fully open.
- Doyun is close enough to carry the emergency folder, not important enough to sit.

Pressure before premise:

- wrong young wrist;
- date stamp showing 1985;
- ink/rubber smell;
- phone bell from inside the meeting room;
- folder edge cutting into the hand;
- adults using him as a folder courier.

Live objects:

- emergency folder;
- disposal cover sheet;
- L/C confirmation request;
- shipping deadline memo;
- Busan payroll memo;
- stamp pad;
- approval sheet.

Starting pressure:

- A disposal/clean-sale route is about to be stamped before the bank sees the hidden exposure.
- If the stamp lands, later verification becomes harder and the family/bank can claim the procedure already moved.

Resisting actors:

- protocol aide protects meeting order and stamp accident liability;
- creditor-bank officer protects bank exposure and audit trail;
- internal family rival protects the clean-sale route and wants the young grandson kept outside.

Protected cost:

- bank liability;
- founder embarrassment;
- sale-route speed;
- meeting control;
- Doyun's corridor access.

Smaller permission:

- Doyun does not ask to stop the sale or explain the future.
- He asks for one 72-hour verification hold line to be inserted before the stamp lands.

Visible objection:

- the bank approval line remains blank or turned sideways;
- the stamp pad closes but does not disappear;
- the protocol aide still keeps Doyun outside the room;
- the rival states that the line is not approval.

Final object position:

- disposal cover is pushed back;
- the memo enters the meeting-folder route;
- stamp has not landed;
- creditor-bank officer holds the folder open at Doyun's line.

Next gate:

- the creditor-bank officer asks who wrote the line;
- this opens the supervised challenge that becomes M02, not trust.

Writer-visible note:

```text
Start outside the creditor-bank meeting, not in a history explanation. Doyun wakes into the wrong young body while holding the emergency folder. Let wrist, folder edge, date stamp, ink smell, and phone bell come before market meaning. He is treated as a family courier, not a decision maker. The disposal cover hides an L/C confirmation request, a shipping deadline memo, and a Busan payroll memo. Doyun adds only one line: hold stamping for 72 hours until the exposure is verified. End with the cover pushed back, the stamp not landing, and the bank officer asking who wrote the line. Nobody praises him.
```

Do not show:

- future-history lecture;
- instant family recognition;
- group rescue;
- the protagonist explaining the whole commercial mechanism;
- the words `reward`, `receipt`, `Writer Context`, `bridge`, `canary`, `harness`.

## 5. Compact Material Unit M02: B2 / EP002

Unit ID:

- `M02 / B2 / EP002 / watched-telex-chair`

Room surface:

- creditor-bank meeting table into telex/export desk.
- The first room still has the blank approval line.
- The second room has one chair that can be opened or withheld.

Pressure before premise:

- the bank officer's pen remains uncapped but not writing approval;
- grandfather does not soften;
- internal rival frames Doyun as a disruption;
- the protocol aide keeps the folder within arm's reach but outside Doyun's possession.

Live objects:

- blank approval line;
- uncapped bank pen;
- telex request form;
- supervised telex chair;
- watch/supervision note;
- access-loss condition line.

Starting pressure:

- The bank cannot approve Doyun's claim without evidence.
- The family cannot ignore the hold without owning the bank exposure if the hidden risk is real.
- Doyun needs one outgoing telex before the clean-sale route resumes.

Resisting actors:

- grandfather protects founder authority and hates being moved by a child;
- creditor-bank officer protects audit trail and bank exposure;
- protocol aide protects meeting liability;
- internal rival protects the disposal route and tries to make the request look like childish obstruction.

Protected cost:

- founder face;
- bank approval liability;
- meeting procedure;
- document access;
- Doyun's right to remain near the folder.

Smaller permission:

- Doyun asks for one outgoing telex under watch, not decision authority.
- He accepts a failure condition: if the buyer continuity and Busan payroll exposure do not prove out, he loses corridor/folder/export-desk access.

Visible objection:

- bank approval line stays blank;
- the supervision note names the watcher;
- the failure condition is written next to his access;
- the chair opens only for this one telex.

Final object position:

- one telex chair is pulled out under supervision;
- the approval line remains blank;
- Doyun's access exists only beside the written failure condition.

Next gate:

- the outgoing telex must produce buyer continuity/cancellation-cost evidence;
- Busan payroll must answer in M03.

Writer-visible note:

```text
Start with distrust still in the room. The bank pen is uncapped but the approval line is blank. Grandfather treats the hold as a nuisance, not insight. Doyun asks for less than belief: one outgoing telex under supervision, plus a written failure condition that strips his access if the reply does not prove the exposure. Let the protocol aide write the watcher and the condition. End with one telex chair pulled out for him while the bank approval line stays blank.
```

Do not show:

- grandfather warming;
- bank belief for free;
- telex access as victory;
- market lecture before the chair opens;
- an abstract "authority movement" ending.

## 7. Director Audit Before Prose

Pass only if:

- pressure appears before explanation;
- Doyun's body/status wound appears within the first scene surface;
- at least three objects move or remain visibly withheld;
- every adult resists for a practical cost;
- the ask is smaller than victory;
- the ending pays with stamp/chair/line, not praise;
- the next gate comes from the telex chair, not a new teaser.

Fail if:

- the opening starts with International Group history;
- Doyun narrates the whole future;
- the bank officer becomes impressed too early;
- grandfather softens in EP002;
- the final beat says "authority opened" instead of showing stamp/chair/blank line.
