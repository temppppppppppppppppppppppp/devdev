## Pitch Truth

One-line premise:

> 회귀한 구조조정 실사관 한도윤은 모두가 청산가치만 보는 부도 회사에서 아직 살아 있는 인증, 보험금, 납품권, 운송 노선, 숙련 인력 기억을 헐값에 사들여 회생 포트폴리오 제국을 만든다.

Reader promise:

- The reader watches a self-interested professional turn bankrupt companies into visible authority, cashflow, and deal access.
- Each win must prove three things in-scene: Do-yoon found a concealed live right, acquired or protected it through a clean receipt, and converted it into authority or cashflow inside the same block.
- The protagonist does not need to be kind or cruel. His default filter is profit, efficiency, legal protection, speed, leverage, and next-gate position.

Why now:

- High-rate pressure, PF cleanup, logistics cost pressure, small manufacturing failures, and workout sales make bankrupt companies feel contemporary.
- The premise gives a money fantasy that is not stock picking: the pleasure is finding living rights inside dead companies.
- The domain naturally produces fast webnovel receipts: meeting seats, data-room access, insurance recognition, exclusive diligence, priority negotiation, transport orders, and next data-room invitations.

Protagonist:

- Name: 한도윤
- Age: 34
- Position: contract restructuring due-diligence examiner at a major accounting firm's restructuring unit.
- Public label: `트럭 사진이나 찍는 실사관`
- Real competence: field-first restructuring professional who reads operating records, contracts, insurance clauses, delivery logs, and neglected staff memory as a bundle of recoverable rights.
- Regression edge: he remembers one catastrophic missed liquidation from the previous life, but future memory only points him toward targets. Current proof must come from present documents, field logs, witnesses, and legal receipts.

Ability constraints:

- Requires actual documents, logs, contracts, or field witnesses.
- Does not read minds.
- Does not predict stock, coin, lottery, or macro prices.
- Does not print value through a fantasy UI.
- The right is not money until he wins authority, proves transferability, handles counter-moves, and gets a clean receipt.
- He does not secretly buy a live advisory client's asset. Acquisition must be clean through advisory exit, public bid, creditor approval, court schedule, or SPV separation.

Core engine:

1. Find a bankrupt company everyone reads as scrap.
2. Identify the living rights inside the dead company.
3. Prove the right through present documents, logs, clauses, or external witnesses.
4. Win a same-block receipt: meeting seat, data-room access, insurance recognition, exclusivity, priority negotiation, or signing authority.
5. Acquire the right bundle cheaply through a clean channel.
6. Convert it into cashflow or authority.
7. Use that receipt to enter a larger distressed deal.

First deal:

- Target: 냉장 물류 회사 `삼진콜드`
- Wrong market reading: broken refrigerated trucks, unpaid wages, electricity arrears, bank collateral, scrap-sale urgency.
- Do-yoon's reading: the trucks are weak, but cold-chain certification, dawn delivery route, recall insurance claim, port cold-storage priority use, and driver route memory are alive.
- First acquisition principle: buy the rights that produce control, not the visible dead burden.

Season 1 arc bank:

- Arc 1: 삼진콜드 cold-chain rights bundle.
- Arc 2: bankrupt food factory with HACCP certification, OEM approval, equipment lease succession, and a veteran line chief.
- Arc 3: failing institutional meal supplier with school/hospital contracts and purchasing rights.
- Arc 4: reverse-logistics startup with seller data, inspection process, and warehouse lease clauses.
- Arc 5: medical-device distributor with permits, hospital maintenance contracts, and recurring consumable orders.
- Arc 6: large PEF attempt to absorb Do-yoon's turnaround portfolio.
- Arc 7: independent distressed-company restructuring manager launch.

TR pacing contract for this work:

- One TR block is a planning bundle that unfolds into roughly 2-6 episodes.
- Every TR block must contain at least two distinct incident beats:
  - one primary proof, deal, control, or conflict incident, and
  - one secondary counter-move, observer shift, receipt, next-gate token, or different operational incident.
- A block cannot be only one scene, one problem, one solution, or one receipt.
- Every block must close with a receipt or sharper leverage change that pays Do-yoon's self-interest.
- Do-yoon saves operations only when saving them is cheaper, faster, more profitable, more defensible, or gives him the next position.

Self-interest law:

- Do-yoon's base line is: `살릴 가치가 있으면 산다. 없으면 자른다.`
- He acts for profit, efficiency, survival, retaliation, legal protection, speed, leverage, or position gain.
- People are kept when they preserve certification, route memory, claims defense, operating speed, or bargaining power.
- Charity rescue, saintly reform, and emotional company-saving are prohibited.

Contamination guard:

- Do not turn Do-yoon into a good-hearted rescuer of all bankrupt companies.
- Do not make the story an illegal insider-trading fantasy.
- Do not let regression memory replace present proof.
- Do not make stock prophecy, coin prophecy, lottery, or generic market prediction the engine.
- Do not import donor names, exact scene order, exact contract surfaces, or proper nouns.
- Do not let family succession become the main reward.
- Do not pay blocks with praise only. Receipts must be authority, access, legal proof, cashflow, protection, leverage, or next-gate position.

- Do-yoon regresses with the memory of a company he recommended for liquidation being sold later for forty times the scrap value.
- In the present, he is sent to photograph `삼진콜드` before a quick scrap sale.
- Everyone sees broken refrigerated trucks. Do-yoon sees an unexpired cold-chain certification, a dawn delivery route, a recall insurance trigger, and port cold-storage priority use.
- He does not say he knows the future. He asks for temperature logs, insurance clauses, delivery claim records, and port allocation sheets.

Episodes 1 to 3 impact:

- Episode 1: regression memory, current humiliation, data-room denial, and the first hint that the trucks are not the asset.
- Episode 2: Do-yoon uses temperature logs and delivery claims to force creditor attention, gaining meeting-seat and limited data-room access.
- Episode 3: field re-inspection and insurance clause proof create a bank direct line and an insurer preliminary recognition email.

First block problem:

- 삼진콜드 will be scrapped within the week.
- If it is sold as scrap, certification, routes, insurance claims, port priority use, and staff route memory will be scattered.
- Do-yoon lacks meeting seat, data access, acquisition authority, and legal separation from advisory work.
- He must convert present proof into clean access and priority before the asset disappears.

First block reward:

- creditor committee meeting seat
- 24-hour limited data-room access
- insurer preliminary recognition email
- bank restructuring direct line
- ten-day exclusive diligence window
- conditional public-bid participation
- priority negotiation and turnaround plan submission right
- first temporary transport order
- next distressed food-factory data-room invitation

Proof scene:

- The bank manager opens the meeting room, and the insurer stops treating him as a disposable field examiner.
