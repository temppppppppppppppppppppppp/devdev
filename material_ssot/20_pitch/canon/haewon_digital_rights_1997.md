## Pitch Truth

One-line premise:

> A 2036 rights-investment and restructuring operator wakes up in 1997 as the disregarded second son of doomed Haewon Group; while everyone rushes to dump broken subsidiaries during the IMF crisis, he buys the discarded contracts, options, agency rights, cable territories, content rights, payment rails, and logistics bottlenecks that will become Korea's digital distribution gates.

Reader promise:

- The reader watches a self-interested operator turn abandoned rights into visible control.
- Each win must prove three things in-scene: Do-yoon found a hidden right, proved its value with current evidence, and converted it into seat, access, equity, cashflow, protection, or a next-gate ticket.
- The protagonist does not need to be good or evil. His default filter is benefit, efficiency, recovery rate, and monopoly potential.

Why now:

- Current platform signals reward clear chaebol, company, professional-proof, abandoned-asset, money-game, entertainment/content, and tech-gateway hooks.
- The 1997 IMF frame gives immediate pressure: workout, creditors, fire sales, family succession panic, and contracts being dumped before anyone understands their future value.
- The sector gives an all-sector monopoly ladder without needing generic stock prophecy: cable, PCS, games, music, payment, logistics, portal, ISP, mobile content, advertising.

Protagonist:

- Name: 강도윤
- Age: 29 at start
- Body/status: second son of Haewon Group, outside the official succession line.
- Original life: 2036 rights investor and restructuring operator.
- Public label: "워크아웃 와중에 돌아온 문제아 차남."
- Real competence: reads broken businesses as rights, obligations, bottlenecks, cashflow claims, and future gateways.
- Supernatural edge: future knowledge exists because of the possession premise, but scenes must show present documents, contracts, field proof, and negotiation receipts.

Core engine:

1. Find a dumped asset or contract in Haewon Group's workout list.
2. Separate living rights from dead liabilities.
3. Prove the right's present value through documents, field bottlenecks, creditor math, or counterparty behavior.
4. Demand a risky seat, option, right, access pass, or separate SPC structure.
5. Convert the receipt into the next sector gate.
6. Repeat until scattered rights become a digital distribution monopoly.

First battlefield:

- Target: Haewon Group's non-core disposal list.
- Wrong market reading: "old cable lines, surplus handsets, useless game/music contracts, messy logistics, obsolete terminals."
- Do-yoon's reading: cable territory, PCS agency priority, content distribution, VAN/PG terminals, logistics terminals, and portal/ISP letters are not trash. They are future tollgates.

TR pacing contract for this work:

- One TR block is a planning bundle that unfolds into roughly 2-6 episodes.
- Every TR block must contain at least two distinct incident beats:
  - one primary right/proof/control incident,
  - and one secondary counter-move, side acquisition, observer shift, or next-gate incident.
- A block cannot be only "discover problem -> think about problem."
- Every block must close with a same-block receipt or a leverage-changing danger.
- The protagonist's choice logic must remain self-interested: profit, efficiency, defense, retaliation, survival, monopoly, or position gain.

Contamination guard:

- Do not turn Do-yoon into a good-hearted family savior.
- Do not let stock prophecy, coin prophecy, lottery, or generic future-news recall replace rights execution.
- Do not import donor names, scene order, exact contract surfaces, or proper nouns.
- Do not let family approval become the main reward. Family recognition is a byproduct of control.
- Do not declare all-sector monopoly at once. It must accumulate through gateways.

## Early Conversion

Opening spike:

- Haewon Group's workout disposal meeting treats cable, PCS, content, payment, and logistics rights as scrap.
- Do-yoon asks for the disposal list and document-room access, not family trust.
- He identifies a cable territory clause that blocks a fire sale and exposes the first hidden gateway.

First three-episode conversion:

- Episode 1: possession shock, workout meeting, public dismissal, document-room access demand.
- Episode 2: cable territory proof, fire-sale interruption, creditor observer shift.
- Episode 3: PCS agency priority proof, broker counter-move, temporary audit seat.

First block receipt:

- Do-yoon wins document-room access, sale hold authority, and a temporary audit seat.
- A creditor observer has to report that the problem son saw a right the official managers missed.
- The family is not proud. That is fine. The leverage changed.

Secondary incident requirement:

- The opening block must not stop at discovery.
- It also needs a family counter-move, creditor reevaluation, broker interference, field witness shift, or next-right unlock before the block closes.
