## Pitch Truth

One-line premise:

> 2026년 플랫폼 결제 전략가였던 강재현은 1997년 IMF 직후 태림그룹 방계 손자의 몸에 빙의해, 모두가 팔아치우려는 PCS 지분, 기지국 유지보수권, 단말 유통망, 카드 청구망을 하나의 `월 청구서 게이트`로 묶어 통신에서 금융, 게임, 쇼핑, 광고까지 모든 섹터의 입장료를 받는 제국을 만든다.

Reader promise:

- The reader watches a self-interested operator turn discarded telecom scraps into the gate every later sector must pass through.
- Each win must prove three things in-scene: 강재현 found a bottleneck others mispriced, converted it through present proof, and received a countable authority/access/billing token inside the same block.
- The protagonist does not need to be kind or evil. His default filter is profit, speed, control, legal defense, leverage, and next-gate position.

Why now:

- Current Korean modern-fantasy money surfaces reward clear business-power hooks: chaebol, company, investment, office authority, big asset discovery, regression/possession edge, and fast status receipts.
- The 1997~2002 telecom turn gives a historical monopoly ladder without relying on stock prophecy: phone number, handset, network maintenance, monthly bill, microbilling, portal account, commerce, advertising, and finance.
- This sector supports "all-sector monopoly" better than a single product because the gate is not a product. It is identity plus billing plus distribution.

Protagonist:

- Name: 강재현
- Age: 29 in body; mental source is a 2026 platform payment strategist.
- Device: possession into 1997 after an unexplained death in 2026.
- Position: ignored branch grandson of fictional `태림그룹`, assigned to a restructuring TF as the person who marks assets for sale.
- Public label: `폐품 목록에 줄 긋는 방계 도련님`
- Real competence: platform-payment operator who reads phone numbers, monthly bills, handset channels, and maintenance contracts as a single gate.
- Possession edge: he knows the broad platform ladder from PCS to mobile content to payment to commerce, but present proof must come from contracts, field logs, carrier calls, and billing documents.

Core group and sector:

- Group: fictional `태림그룹`
- Starting year: 1997, IMF restructuring pressure
- Core subsidiaries and scraps:
  - `태림전선`: cable and base-station maintenance crews
  - `태림전자`: low-end handset OEM line and warehouse inventory
  - `태림카드`: billing and payment relationship
  - `태림유통`: stores and regional sales channel
  - `태림미디어`: PC communication and early content relationships
  - failed PCS consortium option and residual voting rights
- Sector choice:
  - telecom/mobile billing gate
  - the engine can expand into content, games, shopping, finance, advertising, enterprise messaging, data center, logistics, and platform identity.

Ability constraints:

- No mind reading.
- No stock, coin, lottery, or status-window solution.
- Future knowledge points toward the ladder, but every deal needs present proof.
- A right is not real until 강재현 wins a receipt: seat, access, freeze, pilot code, order, voting proxy, settlement fee, legal memo, or next acquisition option.
- He does not keep assets for nostalgia. He keeps only what strengthens phone number, network, handset, billing, distribution, settlement, or data control.

Core engine:

1. Find a discarded telecom-side asset during IMF restructuring.
2. Identify whether it touches phone number, network maintenance, handset acquisition, monthly billing, distribution, settlement, or user data.
3. Prove the hidden gate through present documents, logs, contracts, field capability, or carrier pain.
4. Win a same-block receipt: meeting seat, asset freeze, data-room access, maintenance order, distribution test, billing pilot, voting proxy, or acquisition option.
5. Bundle the asset into `태림모바일서비스`.
6. Convert it into a repeating fee or control right.
7. Use that receipt to enter a wider sector.

First deal:

- Target: 태림그룹's PCS residual option, 태림전선 maintenance unit, 태림전자 low-end handset inventory, 태림카드 billing relationship, and 태림유통 regional store channel.
- Wrong market reading: IMF scraps that should be sold separately.
- 강재현's reading: separately these are junk; together they are a phone-number and monthly-bill gate.
- First acquisition principle: buy and freeze the bottleneck rights before buying scale.

Season 1 arc bank:

- Arc 1: PCS residual rights, maintenance SLA, handset inventory, and billing pilot.
- Arc 2: PC communication provider conversion into phone-number login and portal account.
- Arc 3: ringtone, ARS, character, and mobile game settlement fee.
- Arc 4: card billing and handset micro-payment finance.
- Arc 5: retail and phone-order commerce through 태림유통.
- Arc 6: enterprise messaging, ads, data center, and customer data.
- Arc 7: 2002 World Cup mobile identity and national billing gate expansion.

TR pacing contract for this work:

- One TR block is a planning bundle that unfolds into roughly 2-6 serialized episodes.
- Every TR block must contain at least two distinct incident beats:
  - one primary proof, deal, control, or conflict incident, and
  - one secondary counter-move, observer shift, receipt, next-gate token, or different operational incident.
- A block cannot be only one scene, one problem, one solution, or one receipt.
- Every block must close with a receipt or sharper leverage change that pays 강재현's self-interest.
- 강재현 saves an asset, team, or subsidiary only when saving it is cheaper, faster, more profitable, more defensible, or gives him the next gate.

Self-interest law:

- 강재현's base line is: `쓸모가 있으면 묶고, 없으면 판다.`
- He acts for profit, efficiency, survival, retaliation, legal protection, speed, leverage, control, or position gain.
- People are kept when they preserve maintenance capacity, billing continuity, carrier access, store execution, legal defense, or settlement speed.
- Charity rescue, saintly group revival, and emotional company-saving are prohibited.

- Everyone sees failed PCS option, cable maintenance liabilities, handset inventory, card billing paperwork, and store leases.

- Episode 1: possession, current humiliation, restructuring sale list, and the first realization that the bill is more valuable than the hardware.
- Episode 2: 강재현 uses PCS option and maintenance SLA documents to halt the separate sale and enter the restructuring room.
- Episode 3: field proof at a base-station failure turns a closing maintenance unit into a carrier pilot order and direct procurement line.

- He must convert present proof into a freeze and access before the assets disappear.

- 72-hour asset-sale freeze
