## 1. Critical Pacing Decision

EP001 must not be pure B1 setup.

Pure B1 would mean:

- future ruin;
- body confusion;
- family/status explanation;
- no current-world payment.

That repeats the weak-opening failure.

Therefore:

- EP001 consumes B1 plus the first bite of B2.
- EP002 consumes the rest of B2 plus B3.
- EP003 consumes B4 and opens B5.

Every episode must end with a visible current-world movement.

## 3. EP001 - 파일이 먼저 움직인다

Function:

- ignite regression;
- establish Minjun's current role in the first 300-500 characters after return;
- pay the episode with file/envelope/route movement.

Core surfaces:

- future: speed-storage room, unpaid care text, liquidation report;
- present: Taerim Culture Foundation reception back area, VIP registration file, interpretation brief envelope, family name badge.

Core sequence:

1. Future ruin through objects, not summary.
2. Regression through wrist/date/body/event-noise mismatch.
3. Current role: chairman's third grandson used as event furniture and registration help.
4. Anomaly: a VIP is marked as donor, but the brief uses creditor vocabulary such as covenant, collateral, cross-default, waiver.
5. Resistance: secretary/route staff protects event liability and chairman face.
6. Action: Minjun writes one narrow memo warning that the donor route may expose the chairman to creditor questions.
7. Receipt: envelope label changes, VIP route line moves, memo goes to the top of the event file.

Suggested line family:

- `후원자는 이런 단어를 안 씁니다.`
- `동선을 바꾸자는 게 아닙니다. 회장님이 답할 준비가 된 동선으로 바꾸자는 겁니다.`
- `틀리면 제 이름은 빼셔도 됩니다. 맞으면 파일 위에만 올려 두세요.`

End hook:

- The secretary does not praise him.
- A staffer says the chairman wants to know why Minjun recognized that name.

Must avoid:

- future-knowledge lecture;
- family tree dump;
- smug protagonist;
- instant ally secretary;
- mood-only ending.

## 4. EP002 - 조건이 붙은 계좌

Function:

- turn first proof into conditioned access;
- show rational adult caution;
- pay with limited money and staff access, not affection.

Core sequence:

1. Start with the EP001 memo physically on the chairman-side table.
2. Chairman/secretary test whether Minjun overheard, guessed, or intended to embarrass adults.
3. Minjun narrows proof: donor brief and creditor terms mismatch; if ignored, the chairman faces debt-pressure questions in donor route.
4. Someone asks if he wants money.
5. Minjun refuses broad money and asks for the smallest executable surface.
6. Conditions: 30-day limited account, loss limit, weekly report, 3 staff interviews, no family-name external borrowing.
7. Receipt: chairman memo and staff list enter Minjun's hands.

Suggested line family:

- `돈을 달라는 게 아닙니다. 틀릴 수 있는 크기로만 보겠습니다.`
- `믿어 달라는 말은 안 하겠습니다. 한도와 기간을 붙여 주십시오.`
- `사람 셋이면 됩니다. 제가 고르고, 틀리면 제 메모도 같이 버리시면 됩니다.`

End hook:

- The staff list contains an exit/interview mark beside a discarded overseas-bond analyst.

Must avoid:

- chairman moved by affection;
- giant seed money;
- future market lecture;
- abstract "earned authority" narration.

## 5. EP003 - 계약으로 붙는 사람

Function:

- prevent helper sentimentality;
- convert limited account into a tiny execution cell;
- end on tradable-price proof, not profit.

Core sequence:

1. Staff interview surface opens.
2. First two interviews are unsuitable without wasting pages.
3. Third interview: overseas-bond analyst with a rejected liquidity/rate report and resignation slot.
4. Analyst resistance: career risk, attribution risk, family denial risk.
5. Minjun offers contract terms: loss backstop, performance bonus, NDA, attribution protection.
6. Task remains narrow: get executable external quote under specified limit.
7. Receipt: analyst delays resignation, rejected report moves to trial-cell file, first bank contact is made.
8. B5 entry: bank replies with strict margin, maturity, custody, and size conditions.

Suggested line family:

- `제 편이 되라는 말이 아닙니다. 틀렸을 때 당신 이름이 먼저 타지 않게 하자는 겁니다.`
- `보고서가 맞았다는 증명은 나중입니다. 먼저 체결 가능한 가격이 있는지부터 봅니다.`
- `큰돈은 필요 없습니다. 거절당하지 않을 만큼 작은 조건이면 됩니다.`

End hook:

- The bank sends an executable quote or conditional term sheet.
- It is not a win yet, but now the judgment has a price.

Must avoid:

- analyst emotionally impressed;
- bank easy terms;
- profit landing in EP003;
- long NDF mechanics lecture.
