# Firefly B1-B2 Micro-Canary Before 70 Harness v1

Date: 2026-05-17
Status: active operator harness
Scope: material-side candidates expected to feed Firefly S2-S3-S4 or close writer-facing manuscript production

## 1. Purpose

This harness exists to prevent the wrong expansion unit.

A pitch can look commercially promising, and a Phase0/TR plan can look coherent, while still failing when Firefly S4 tries to write human-like prose. The usual failure is not missing idea. It is that the writer receives a 70-block sector map before the opening scene has proven reader attachment, room pressure, visible proof, and paid receipt.

Therefore:

`Do not generate full 70 production TR before a B1-B2 micro-canary proves the opening can become scene-native S4 material.`

## 2. Operating Rule

For any new material that is meant to become Firefly prose:

1. Select or lock the canon-candidate premise.
2. Translate it through the Firefly S4 scene-native bridge.
3. Build a B1-B2 micro-canary packet.
4. Run a Firefly/S4-style prose dry run or equivalent close manuscript sample.
5. Perform director readback plus adversarial/vicarious-satisfaction audit.
6. Patch the seed/work_guard if it fails.
7. Only after pass, expand into Phase0 and then TR 1-10.
8. Do not create TR 70 until at least TR 1-10 proves pacing and the first tranche audit passes.

This harness is stricter than "make a planning memo." The opening must be tested as writing pressure before the range is multiplied.

## 3. Unit Definitions

### B1

B1 is the opening pressure and ignition unit.

It must establish:

- protagonist body/status disturbance;
- current room or work surface;
- why the protagonist's present seat costs money, name, family standing, job, access, survival, or dignity;
- the first object/document/person that makes the crisis visible;
- no grand victory yet.

B1 may pay only a small ignition receipt: the reader understands why this protagonist must move now.

### B2

B2 is the first proof and reclassification unit.

It must show:

- one narrow protagonist action that only this protagonist can take now;
- one rational adult/system blocker;
- one visible object, procedure, route, access, title, chair, call, or file movement;
- one dismissive adult or system changing behavior because of protagonist proof;
- a named next gate.

B2 must pay at least a small reader receipt. The minimum feeling is:

`The people who dismissed him changed procedure because of him.`

### EP001 Combined Micro-Canary

For regression/chaebol works, B1 and B2 may be compressed into one EP001 micro-canary of roughly 1,500-2,500 Korean characters.

This is allowed only if the sample still contains both:

- ignition/role clarity;
- first proof/reclassification/access movement.

## 4. Pass Criteria

A B1-B2 micro-canary passes only if all are true:

- It reads as scene prose, not report, doctrine, outline, or harness.
- The reader can price the protagonist's current situation.
- A current-scene object/action changes the room or system.
- At least one dismissive adult/system pays visible behavior cost.
- The receipt appears before the sample ends.
- Future knowledge or technical evidence buys only a small time/access/reclassification edge, not a full victory.
- The next gate is named as a person, room, file, call, meeting, account, badge, deadline, or route.
- No famous-person ladder, world-historical lecture, or long sector list enters the opening sample.

If any pass criterion fails, do not expand to 70. Patch the seed or work_guard first.

## 5. Fail Signals

Treat any of the following as a failed micro-canary:

- premise/world-history lecture before current scene pressure;
- technical document with no human behavior change;
- reward exists only as internal monologue or narrator claim;
- the first win is a giant profit or full group rescue;
- the protagonist is humiliated passively with no ownership move;
- future knowledge appears as omniscient lecture rather than scar, recognition, or document handling;
- the opening names Jobs, Musk, Son, Apple, Tesla, or global ladder before the first local receipt;
- the next gate is an abstract promise rather than a concrete door.

## 6. Relation To 70 Blocks

This harness does not ban long-range planning.

Allowed before micro-canary pass:

- thin sector opportunity atlas;
- gate matrix;
- first two-arc research checklist;
- opening Phase0 seed;
- B1-B2 or EP001 micro-canary packet.

Not allowed before micro-canary pass:

- production TR 70;
- production BI;
- final Phase0 declaration;
- immediate-use or range-complete claim.

After micro-canary pass:

- draft or restart the proper Phase0;
- freeze or patch work_guard;
- generate only TR 1-10 first;
- audit the tranche before wider range.
