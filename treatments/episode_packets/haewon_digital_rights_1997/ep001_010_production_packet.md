## 0. Packet Contract

- 1 TR block expands to 2-6 episodes. This packet uses B1=2, B2=3, B3=3, B4=2.
- Every episode must carry at least two beats: a primary business incident plus a secondary complication, witness move, pressure move, or reversal.
- Kang Do-yoon does not ask to be loved. Recognition must come from a table, contract, document room permission, audit seat, field witness, or SPC draft.
- The protagonist's reward engine is profit, efficiency, defense, optionality, and rights control.
- Future knowledge stays private. Public proof must come from current documents: original disposal lists, side contracts, subsidy tables, renewal sheets, field data, and cashflow tables.
- No episode may end on abstract praise. The receipt must be access, delay, official memo, review right, audit seat, field witness, or corporate draft.

## 2. Episode Plans

### EP001 - 버릴 거면 제가 봅니다

Source: TR B1, first half.

Primary incident:

- Kang Do-yoon wakes in the Haewon Group annex in 1997, with IMF news running and a disposal agenda already on the family table.
- He does not spend the opening episode explaining reincarnation. He studies what the family is about to sell.

Secondary incident:

- Kang Min-seok and the executives frame cable SO, electronic parts inventory, game distribution, and music masters as cleanup junk.
- Do-yoon notices that everyone is naming companies while the buried value sits in rights, regions, renewal clauses, and future connection gates.

Receipt:

- Do-yoon interrupts the quick-sale rhythm by asking for the original disposal list and contract storage box for one day.
- The episode closes before approval, with the family forced to answer a specific document request rather than dismiss a vague objection.

Next hook:

- The document room becomes the first battlefield.

### EP002 - 원본 하루

Source: TR B1, second half.

Primary incident:

- Do-yoon points to two current-document blanks: regional cable transmission rights and a PCS priority notation error.
- He never mentions the future. He makes the present paperwork embarrass the quick-sale plan.

Secondary incident:

- Kang Tae-jun resents the interruption because it slows the family response to creditors.
- Seo In-ha watches whether Do-yoon can convert a strange claim into verifiable document work.

Receipt:

- Tae-jun grants one day of original-list access.
- The sale meeting is delayed, and Seo In-ha writes Do-yoon's name into a creditor memo for the first time.

Next hook:

- Do-yoon opens the Haewon Cable disposal file and sees a right that is being priced like scrap equipment.

### EP003 - 케이블은 설비가 아닙니다

Source: TR B2, first third.

Primary incident:

- In the document room, Do-yoon finds a Haewon Cable file that looks ugly on the balance sheet: old equipment, shrinking subscribers, and debt.
- The side contract tells a different story: a specific regional transmission right and a future data-line priority clause.

Secondary incident:

- Min-seok's line pushes to finish the cable sale before the creditor report.
- Their logic is clean on the surface: old cable assets must be sold while anyone still wants them.

Receipt:

- Do-yoon challenges the sale price with a contract clause rather than a speech.
- The cable file is pulled out of the bulk disposal stack for a separate check.

Next hook:

- He must prove that the right and the equipment need different prices.

### EP004 - 권리와 설비를 갈라놓다

Source: TR B2, second third.

Primary incident:

- Do-yoon places the transmission clause and regional exclusivity term next to the sale table.
- He argues that selling the company can still happen, but the right cannot be thrown in as a free attachment.

Secondary incident:

- Executives attack the move as delay tactics from a problem son who does not understand workout pressure.
- Seo In-ha presses for a creditor-facing explanation, not family-room intuition.

Receipt:

- A rights/equipment separation table is accepted for review.
- Do-yoon's demand becomes a pricing question, not a personality problem.

Next hook:

- The creditor report must now carry a rights-separation line.

### EP005 - 보류 도장

Source: TR B2, final third.

Primary incident:

- Seo In-ha adds a rights-separation revaluation item to the creditor report.
- The Haewon Cable sale paper receives a hold stamp.

Secondary incident:

- Min-seok's line loses speed but not authority; they can still call the delay a one-off.
- Do-yoon needs the next right to prove the pattern scales beyond cable.

Receipt:

- One-day access has become sale-hold leverage.
- Seo recognizes the measurable benefit: Do-yoon did not ask for trust, he left a repricing item the creditors can use.

Next hook:

- The electronic-parts file bundles PCS terminal inventory with dealership-network priority rights.

### EP006 - 대리점망 한 줄

Source: TR B3, first third.

Primary incident:

- The electronic-parts disposal file bundles PCS terminal inventory and dealership contracts into one sale package.
- Park Gi-moon says a whole-package sale is the fastest way to reduce inventory loss.

Secondary incident:

- Ryu Han-gyu approaches Min-seok with a cheap offer that appears to absorb the inventory risk.
- The offer is actually a way to take the dealership-network priority line at a distorted price.

Receipt:

- Do-yoon separates terminal inventory from dealership-network priority rights in the working table.
- The sale is no longer a simple inventory cleanout.

Next hook:

- He must prove the dealership network is worth more than the terminals.

### EP007 - 재고보다 비싼 판매망

Source: TR B3, second third.

Primary incident:

- Do-yoon cross-checks telecom subsidies against dealership contract renewal sheets.
- The numbers show that the network position, not the physical terminals, is the valuable asset.

Secondary incident:

- Ryu realizes Do-yoon is not randomly blocking sales; he is hunting rights.
- That makes every later right more expensive and more contested.

Receipt:

- The creditor-side proof closes the immediate price dispute.
- The dealership right becomes a next-gate asset rather than a hidden freebie in the inventory bundle.

Next hook:

- Do-yoon can ask for a formal audit position, but the ask will expose him further.

### EP008 - 임시 감사 좌석

Source: TR B3, final third.

Primary incident:

- Seo In-ha proposes a temporary audit seat because Do-yoon's table changed the creditor-side view of the sale.
- Do-yoon uses the opening to request standing document-room access and electronic-parts sale review authority.

Secondary incident:

- Ryu Han-gyu watches the authority move and begins tracking what Do-yoon is collecting.
- Do-yoon wins a seat but creates an outside competitor who understands the pattern.

Receipt:

- Temporary audit seat, standing document access, and electronic-parts sale review right are secured.
- Recognition lands as permission and review authority, not praise.

Next hook:

- With standing access, Do-yoon enters the junk contract box: games, music masters, and cable ad packages.

### EP009 - 팔리지 않는 노래와 게임

Source: TR B4, first half.

Primary incident:

- Do-yoon opens scattered files: game distribution contracts, music master rights, cable regional ad packages, and PC room supply channels.
- He builds one cashflow table from rights everyone else has treated as unrelated clutter.

Secondary incident:

- Park Gi-moon laughs that the contracts eat management cost and produce no immediate money.
- Min-seok attacks the pattern as a threat to workout recovery because Do-yoon seems to be collecting useless rights for himself.

Receipt:

- O Se-gwan is pulled in to verify field ad slots and regional network data.
- The table gains a field witness, preventing the scene from feeling like secret future knowledge.

Next hook:

- The witness must confirm enough field data to justify a separate SPC draft.

### EP010 - 첫 상품 묶음

Source: TR B4, second half.

Primary incident:

- O Se-gwan confirms that the cable ad slot, PC room channel, game distribution, and music rights can be treated as one access-right product.
- Do-yoon frames content rights as the first product that turns connection access into money.

Secondary incident:

- Min-seok's line cannot dismiss the rights as junk once a field witness and cashflow table exist.
- The more Do-yoon collects, the easier it becomes to accuse him of diverting good rights into a personal structure.

Receipt:

- A separate SPC approval draft is written.
- Content rights are reclassified from miscellaneous contracts into the first monetizable access-right bundle.

Next hook:

- The B5 battlefield opens with the accusation that Do-yoon is taking the good rights for himself; he must split group-loss defense from his own priority negotiation right.

## 3. Continuity Locks

- EP001-EP010 uses TR B1-B4 only. B5 appears only as the next hook.
- Do-yoon's public proof comes from current records: disposal list, contract storage box, cable side contract, subsidy table, dealership renewal sheet, field ad slot, regional network data, and cashflow table.
- Recognition witnesses are Kang Tae-jun, Seo In-ha, Ryu Han-gyu, and O Se-gwan, but none of them reward Do-yoon with praise. They reward him with access, a memo, audit authority, and a draft.
- The protagonist is supportable because he creates calculable benefit for the institution while still pursuing his own rights-control position.
- The opening arc must feel like the reader watching a dismissed second son become unavoidable through receipts.
