Secondary incident:

- Noh Eun-chae demands that Do-yoon name the loss plainly before asking owners to stay.

Protagonist logic:

- Do-yoon does not call the loss temporary noise. He fixes the loss amount first because only measured loss can become enforceable breach proof.

2. Owners report lost seats, refund calls, and angry users.

Receipt:

- The loss is officially counted instead of softened.
- The packet opens with a real defeat, but the data is now usable.

End hook:

- Counting the loss is not enough; the logs must prove who breached the SLA.
