## 0. Packet Contract

- 1 TR block expands to 2-6 episodes. This packet uses B1=2, B2=3, B3=3, B4=2.
- Every episode must carry at least two beats: a primary rights/proof incident plus a secondary pressure, witness, field proof, or next-gate move.
- Seo Do-yoon does not win by predicting stocks, worshipping dot-com nostalgia, or saving a failing startup out of kindness.
- Public proof must come from current materials: IR documents, IDC arrears, PG test clauses, domain escrow records, server logs, routing partitions, PC-room line records, and contract drafts.

- Recognition is a receipt-bearing calculation. Baek Seung-min, Min Ji-a, the ClickSquare CFO, and Oh Jun-hyuk must reward proof with access, wording, records, or authority, not abstract praise.

## 2. Episode Plans

### EP001 - 회사는 안 삽니다

Source: TR B1, first half.

Primary incident:

- ClickSquare's pre-listing IR room expects Taesung Telecom money, and Jang Tae-oh sells the room on valuation, user count, and a closing window.
- Seo Do-yoon breaks the investment rhythm by saying he is not buying the company or the listing premium.

Secondary incident:

- Baek Seung-min frames Do-yoon as a branch analyst with no authority to slow the main telecom strategy.
- Do-yoon finds the buried columns instead: IDC lease arrears, PG test appendix, domain escrow notation, and PC-room line supply rider.

Protagonist logic:

- Do-yoon does not need to prove the future crash. He only needs current documents that make cash investment look expensive and rights collateral look cheap.

Scene order:

1. The IR starts with Jang Tae-oh selling user growth and a pre-listing clock.
2. Baek asks whether Taesung will commit money before the market closes.
3. Do-yoon checks the attachments instead of the valuation page.
4. He says the thesis line: he will not buy the company, only the server-room key and payment rights.
5. The room cannot dismiss him because he has named the exact documents they tried to leave in the appendix.

Receipt:

- The equity-investment rhythm is interrupted.
- Do-yoon wins the first reader reward: not a purchase, but the right to define the object of negotiation.

End hook:

- Baek demands a bounded ask. Do-yoon asks for 72 hours with the original server, PG, domain, and PC-room records.

### EP002 - 72시간 실사권

Source: TR B1, second half.

Primary incident:

- Do-yoon negotiates for 72-hour exclusive diligence before ClickSquare can use Taesung's name to pull other investors back into the room.

Secondary incident:

- Baek accepts the narrow window because it costs less than a cash commitment, but he assigns surveillance and a closing deadline.
- The ClickSquare side tries to exclude the PC-room line rider as a minor operations document.

Protagonist logic:

- The smallest permission unlocks the largest optionality: original access before cash, options before shares, proof before trust.

Scene order:

1. Baek asks whether Do-yoon wants budget, board approval, or a family favor.
2. Do-yoon asks for no cash, no title, and no press release.
3. He names four original materials: IDC lease file, PG test contract, domain escrow confirmation, PC-room line supply rider.
4. ClickSquare pushes back on the PC-room rider.
5. Do-yoon ties it to game distribution and payment proof, forcing it into the diligence list.

Receipt:

- 72-hour exclusive diligence is granted.
- Do-yoon receives original access to the server, PG, domain, and PC-room files without spending Taesung capital.

End hook:

- The first diligence table shows that the IDC lease can be separated from the inflated equity story.

## 4. Prose Direction For Manuscript Runner

- Open scenes late. The meeting, log review, contract wording, or server-room access fight should already be moving when the episode begins.
- Keep Do-yoon's language short, transactional, and proof-facing.
- Make documents into action objects: an appendix pulled forward, a clause rewritten, a rack map split, a log line cut, a badge issued, a node assigned.
- Avoid nostalgia lectures about 2000s internet culture. Period texture is useful only when it changes proof, rights, settlement, or access.
- Every episode ending should pay one visible receipt and then open the next pressure.
- The early reader promise is not "he knows future winners." It is "he buys the rights everyone else misprices while the present documents prove him right."
