### EP011 - 결제 실패의 가격표

Source: TR B5, first half.

Primary incident:

- The PG payment-module night starts with the three PC-room nodes assigned in EP010.
- The test result is ugly: the module drops sessions, produces partial failures, and cannot support an inflated investor story.

Secondary incident:

- Noh Eun-chae worries that failed payment tests will hurt the PC-room operators who agreed to help.
- Baek Seung-min treats the failure as proof that Do-yoon's rights bundle is too messy for Taesung to touch.

Protagonist logic:

- Do-yoon does not hide the low success rate. He uses it to lower the price of the right and to demand liability-cap wording before a wider pilot.

Scene order:

1. The first PC-room node runs the payment-module test after midnight.
2. Two sessions fail, one session reaches payment confirmation, and the log shows exactly where the module breaks.
3. Noh asks whether this means the pilot is over.
4. Do-yoon marks failure type, user path, and refund risk on one table.
5. He says the failed test is the reason they can buy the right cheaply.

Receipt:

- Payment failure becomes a priced proof object, not a dead end.
- Do-yoon gains the leverage needed to demand a limited test right instead of a broad liability burden.

End hook:

- The next fight is wording: who takes responsibility when the limited pilot breaks?
