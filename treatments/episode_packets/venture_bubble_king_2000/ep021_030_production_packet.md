## 2. Episode Plans

### EP021 - 남은 증거의 가격

Source: TR B9, first third.

Primary incident:

- Do-yoon brings the EP020 preserved proof packet to the contract table: domain escrow, log images, PG/PC-room payment copies, and backup-node records.
- The seller and strategy office both try to treat the server-loss aftermath as damaged leverage.

Secondary incident:

- Min Ji-a warns that evidence alone does not become power unless the trigger conditions are written cleanly.
- Baek Seung-min argues that lost hardware should lower Do-yoon's authority, not increase it.

Protagonist logic:

- Do-yoon does not deny the loss. He prices the surviving rights and refuses to buy dead equipment back for pride.

Scene order:

1. The meeting opens on the list of lost server hardware.
2. Do-yoon places the preserved proof packet beside the loss list.
3. The seller asks for money to restore the hardware position.
4. Do-yoon refuses and marks only rights that still produce proof.
5. Min identifies the condition language needed to exercise each right later.

Receipt:

- The preserved evidence is accepted as the basis for conditional option drafting.
- Hardware loss becomes cheaper option pricing instead of a reason to retreat.

End hook:

- The option cannot close without field proof that PC-room operators will participate.
