## 2. Episode Plans

### EP031 - 무료 장비의 가격

Source: TR B13, first half.

Primary incident:

- SpeedLink's free-equipment offer spreads through the PC-room owners before Do-yoon's SLA standard can settle.
- The offer looks good on the surface: free routers, discounted setup, and faster installation.

Secondary incident:

- Noh Eun-chae brings the offer sheet because owners are tempted by visible hardware savings.
- Do-yoon compares it against the PC-room coupon settlement table and finds the hidden cost: 2-year lock-in, log-control loss, and outage immunity.

Protagonist logic:

- Do-yoon does not insult the free equipment. He turns "free" into total cost, log custody, and future outage risk.

Scene order:

1. Noh arrives with owners asking why they should refuse free gear.
2. SpeedLink's sales language names discounts but hides failure responsibility.
3. Do-yoon places the free-equipment offer beside the coupon settlement table.
4. He marks the three invisible prices: term lock, log custody, and outage exemption.
5. The room stops treating the offer as a gift.

Receipt:

- The hidden cost table is exposed in front of PC-room operators.
- The signing rush is interrupted before lock-in closes.

End hook:

- A counteroffer must give operators something real, not just a warning.
