## 0. Packet Contract

- 1 TR block expands to 2-6 episodes. This packet uses B5=2, B6=3, B7=2, B8=3.
- Every episode must carry at least two beats: primary business incident plus secondary complication, witness, pressure, or reversal.
- Han Doyun's reward engine is profit, efficiency, defense, optionality, and position gain.
- He does not save people because he is kind. If people survive, it is because their survival protects a recoverable right or a future cashflow.
- Every episode closes with a concrete receipt or a concrete retained advantage under pressure.
- Family recognition remains limited to permission, review standard, or surveillance. Praise is not the prize.

## 2. Episode Plans

### EP011 - 해지 통보

Source: TR B5, first half.

Primary incident:

- Before the sample delivery can run, the core store landlord sends a termination notice based on unpaid rent and closure rumors.

Secondary incident:

- Jo Taegeon frames the notice as proof that Doyun bought paper rights while losing the physical site.
- Park Munho reacts emotionally, pushing the scene toward rescue rhetoric.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun treats the store as a control point, not a sentimental place.
- If the site disappears, settlement right, trademark use, and sample logistics lose execution shape.

Scene order:

1. The termination notice arrives while the sample delivery memo is being prepared.
2. Jo says the rights table is useless if the store is gone.
3. Park Munho asks whether Doyun can "save the shop."
4. Doyun rejects the word save and asks for arrears, deposit, incoming sales, and sample date.
5. He marks the landlord's threat as a priceable option problem.

Receipt:

- The landlord attack is reframed from emotional emergency to negotiable option math.

End hook:

- The landlord has a higher-paying replacement tenant waiting.

### EP012 - 나가라는 말의 가격

Source: TR B5, second half.

Primary incident:

- Doyun negotiates a 30-day priority negotiation extension with the landlord.

Secondary incident:

- The landlord demands certainty and immediate value, not hope.
- Jo tries to record Doyun's move as accepting rent arrears.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun does not ask for mercy. He buys time only where time can produce repeat cashflow.

Scene order:

1. Doyun places four items on one page: arrears order, deposit offset, trademark-preserved foot traffic, sample delivery date.
2. The landlord pressures for immediate exit or a higher premium.
3. Doyun offers a 30-day priority negotiation extension tied to deposit offset and proof date.
4. Jo tries to add all arrears to Doyun's responsibility.
5. Doyun narrows the signature language to option extension, not debt assumption.

Receipt:

- The landlord signs a 30-day priority negotiation extension.
- The core site becomes an option asset long enough to run the sample delivery.

End hook:

- With the site alive for 30 days, the cafeteria buyer will now judge the delivery under fixed temperature and fixed time.

### EP013 - 온도 기록

Source: TR B6, first third.

Primary incident:

- Doyun prepares the sample delivery proof package for the institutional meal buyer.

Secondary incident:

Protagonist logic:

Scene order:

2. Kang warns that a single truck and a weak store cannot absorb failure.
3. Park Munho tries to talk about taste and history.
4. Doyun cuts that off and demands temperature logs, settlement-account proof, and lease option copy.
5. The proof package is sent ahead of the sample.

Receipt:

- The buyer receives a reliability package instead of a brand appeal.

End hook:

- The buyer replies: one late delivery ends the conversation.

### EP018 - 숨겨진 물류비

Source: TR B8, first third.

3. Giseok asks whether Doyun hid the loss.

### EP019 - 세 갈래 비용

Source: TR B8, middle third.

Primary incident:

- Doyun splits the unpaid logistics fee into three buckets: payable cost, Jo's misallocated cost, and offsettable cost through recurring contract.

Secondary incident:

- Jo fights the misallocation bucket because it points back to his operational responsibility.
- Baek watches Doyun's loss handling more closely than the headline debt amount.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun's goal is not to look undefeated. It is to prevent total liability transfer and keep the recoverable structure alive.

Scene order:

1. Doyun matches the payment-order table with route records.
2. He marks costs that must be paid.
3. He marks costs that were allocated to the wrong store or wrong route.
4. He marks costs that can be offset against the recurring supply contract.
5. Jo realizes the split leaves his past allocation visible.

Receipt:

- The debt is no longer one lump.
- Responsibility control returns to Doyun's table.

End hook:

- Giseok accepts the split only if Doyun can produce a 30-day workout condition without asking for more family capital.

### EP020 - 상계형 워크아웃

Source: TR B8, final third.

Primary incident:

- Doyun secures a 30-day offset workout condition instead of accepting full logistics debt transfer.

Secondary incident:

- His internal capital evaluation is partially cut because the loss was real.
- Baek becomes more interested because Doyun did not hide the loss.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun accepts a controlled scar to keep control. A partial defeat with retained workout terms is better than a clean-looking lie that kills future deal credibility.

Scene order:

1. Giseok states the cost of the debt file: Doyun's available internal credibility is reduced.
2. Jo pushes for full debt assumption.
3. Doyun offers the split table plus 30-day offset workout tied to recurring contract settlement.
4. The full transfer fails. The workout condition is accepted.
5. Baek's interest shifts from gossip to test: this heir handles bad debt without hiding it.

Receipt:

- Doyun blocks full unpaid logistics fee transfer.
- He secures 30-day offset workout terms and keeps responsibility-order control.

End hook:

- To prevent the next attack, Doyun and Sera must turn all separated rights, liabilities, and receipts into one official rights list. EP021 starts TR B9.
