## 0. Packet Contract

- 1 TR block expands to 2-6 episodes. This packet never compresses a block below 2 episodes.
- Every episode must carry at least two beats: a primary business incident and a secondary complication, reversal, witness, or pressure move.
- The protagonist is not good or evil first. Han Doyun chooses profit, efficiency, defense, optionality, and position gain.
- Vicarious reward is concrete: access right, official table entry, cashflow proof, control point, schedule, signed option, or next-asset viewing right.
- Family recognition is a byproduct. Do not make praise, reconciliation, or moral approval the main reward.
- Ability use is document-contact based only: ledger, settlement table, PG account, lease summary, logistics rate sheet, delivery condition sheet. No mind reading, stock prophecy, or miracle market foresight.

## 2. Episode Plans

### EP001 - 버릴 자산

Source: TR B1, first half.

Primary incident:

- Hanseong Food Logistics family dinner treats Park Munho Gukbap's 12 stores as disposal paperwork.
- Executives reduce the asset to closure cost, review risk, unpaid rent, and bad ratings.

Secondary incident:

- Doyun is publicly framed as a rich only son who learned spreadsheets but has no operating authority.
- When his hand touches the summary ledger, the ability shows live fragments behind the dead-looking stores: settlement right, logistics route, trademark use, lease priority option.

Protagonist logic:

- He does not argue that the stores deserve saving.
- He calculates that a dead-looking asset can become cheap if everyone else insists on throwing away the whole bundle.

Scene order:

1. Dinner starts with casual disposal talk.
2. Jo Taegeon or a conservative executive turns the issue into "close it before it smells worse."
3. Doyun touches the ledger, sees the separate rights, and keeps his face still.
4. He asks one narrow question: "If this is trash, who owns the money that still comes in?"
5. The table freezes because he has not asked to buy stores, only to inspect disposal residue.

Receipt:

- The disposal decision is interrupted, not reversed.
- End the episode on Doyun saying that if they will throw it away, he will look at it first.

Forbidden drift:

- Do not make him defend employees, customers, or memories.
- Do not explain the whole ability. Show the ledger-touch signal through one precise perception.

### EP002 - 원본 장부 1회

Source: TR B1, second half.

Primary incident:

- Doyun negotiates for one-time original ledger access before the closure procedure begins.

Secondary incident:

- Han Giseok dislikes the interruption and tests whether Doyun is asking for money, authority, or a toy.
- Yoon Sera is assigned to watch the review, turning her from observer into verifier.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun asks for the smallest permission that unlocks the largest optionality: document access.
- He avoids requesting capital before he has proof.

Scene order:

1. After dinner, Giseok calls Doyun out for interfering without mandate.
2. Doyun offers a narrow condition: no budget request, no operating order, only one look at originals before disposal.
3. Jo tries to laugh it off as an heir's curiosity.
4. Doyun names the exact document classes he needs: original ledger, store-by-store lease summary, settlement table, logistics rate sheet.
5. Giseok grants one viewing window and places Yoon Sera beside him.

Receipt:

- One-time original ledger access is granted.
- The first power shift is access, not cash.

End hook:

- The originals are brought into the strategy room, and the first page has a settlement column the summary never showed.

### EP003 - 가게는 안 삽니다

Source: TR B2, first third.

Primary incident:

- In the strategy room, everyone asks whether Doyun will acquire the stores.
- Doyun identifies the full-store acquisition as a liability trap.

Secondary incident:

- Jo Taegeon frames full acquisition as the only "responsible" move, trying to dump unpaid rent, logistics debt, payroll residue, bad reviews, and closure cost onto Doyun.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun refuses the emotional frame. He does not buy stores. He buys the order in which money arrives.

Scene order:

1. Original documents arrive with Yoon Sera watching.
2. Jo pushes a full acquisition summary across the table.
3. Doyun marks the cost columns in one color and the live rights in another.
4. He says the episode thesis: "가게는 안 삽니다."
5. The room misreads it as retreat until he names the rights separately.

Receipt:

- The liability trap is exposed in front of strategy staff.
- Doyun keeps his capital and reputation from being tied to the whole rotten bundle.

End hook:

- He writes five words on the board: settlement, logistics, trademark, lease option, joint purchase.

### EP004 - 권리만 산다

Source: TR B2, middle third.

Primary incident:

- Doyun builds the rights-only acquisition table: settlement right, logistics supply right, trademark use right, lease priority option, joint purchase right.

Secondary incident:

- Yoon Sera challenges whether the rights can actually be separated under the contracts.
- Jo keeps pushing the unpaid cost pile to blur the separation.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun does not need trust. He needs a table where each liability has a matching owner and each live right has a recoverable cashflow.

Scene order:

1. Doyun sorts each document into "cash comes in," "cash goes out," and "control point."
2. Sera tests him on one clause that appears to bind the rights together.
3. Doyun touches the lease summary and sees the option clause glow through the paper trail.
4. He separates the bundle into rights that can be priced and liabilities that should be rejected.
5. Jo realizes the full acquisition dump is losing shape.

Receipt:

- Sera admits the separation table is at least checkable.
- The deal changes from heir whim to verifiable structure.

End hook:

- The table still needs Giseok's formal permission before the review window closes.

### EP005 - 공식 검토안

Source: TR B2, final third.

Primary incident:

- Doyun presents the rights bundle proposal to Giseok and gets it placed on the official review table.

Secondary incident:

- Giseok forbids full acquisition, limiting Doyun's move, but that restriction actually protects him from Jo's dump.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun accepts the limit because it matches his profit logic. No store acquisition, no inherited mess, only rights with receipts.

Scene order:

2. Jo warns that splitting rights will make Hanseong look irresponsible.
3. Doyun counters with numbers: maximum exposure, recoverable inflow, rejected liability.
4. Giseok says Doyun cannot buy the stores.
5. Doyun answers that he never asked to.

Receipt:

- Rights bundle review is officially allowed for one day.
- Doyun gets his first real deal structure without taking the poisoned store package.

End hook:

- The settlement-right row has a blank account field. Sera notices Doyun noticed it first.

### EP010 - 샘플 배송권

Source: TR B4, second half.

Secondary incident:

- Kang tests whether Doyun understands the operational bottleneck, not just the table.

3. Kang gives a conditional yes: one truck, one slot, no excuses.
4. Jo tries to record it as Doyun accepting logistics loss.
5. Doyun corrects the memo: "sample delivery test right," not "loss acceptance."

Receipt:

- One refrigerated truck and one sample delivery slot are secured.
- The first asset now has a route to recurring contract cashflow.

End hook:

- Before the sample can run, a core landlord sends termination pressure. EP011 starts TR B5.

## 4. Prose Direction For Manuscript Runner

- Open scenes late. The business problem should already be moving when the episode begins.
- Keep Doyun's lines short, specific, and transactional.
- Use documents as action objects: a finger on the ledger, a line highlighted, a table split, a meeting link created, a slot written into a memo.
- Avoid long lectures. If exposition is needed, make it a price, a deadline, or a control right.
- Every episode ending should leave a gained receipt plus the next pressure.
- The early reader promise is not "he will be loved." It is "he sees the recoverable money everyone else mislabels as trash."
