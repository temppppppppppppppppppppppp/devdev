## 0. Packet Contract

- 1 TR block expands to 2-6 episodes. This packet uses B9=2, B10=2, B11=3, B12=3.
- Every episode must carry at least two beats: primary business incident plus secondary complication, witness, pressure, or reversal.
- Han Doyun's decisions remain self-interested: profit, efficiency, defense, optionality, and position gain.
- Opening-arc rewards must convert into institutional language before the next asset. Otherwise the first success feels lucky rather than scalable.
- The cold-chain NPL does not start with acquisition. It starts with viewing rights, raw logs, standstill, and lease-order control.
- No episode may end on abstract praise. The receipt must be access, table adoption, exclusive review, raw source, standstill, right split, or workout condition.

## 2. Episode Plans

### EP021 - 흩어진 권리들

Source: TR B9, first half.

Primary incident:

- After the unpaid logistics fee shock, Doyun and Sera gather the scattered receipts: settlement account, refrigerated route slot, lease option, recurring contract, franchisee deferment, and offset workout.

Secondary incident:

- Conservative family executives reduce the first deal to a lucky small contract with a real loss attached.
- Jo Taegeon tries to hide his responsibility inside the same fog.

Protagonist logic:

- Doyun needs the first deal to become a repeatable internal language, not a story about a lucky heir.
- He removes all "good thing" framing and leaves only price, responsibility, recovery date, bottleneck, and next battlefield.

Scene order:

1. Sera lays out the separate documents and says the executives still see pieces, not a method.
2. Jo calls the recurring contract too small and the logistics debt too large.
3. Doyun turns each document into a row: right, cost, owner, recovery date, control point.
4. Sera notices that even the workout condition can be listed as a protected right.
5. The draft rights list is ready for the next strategy-room upload.

Receipt:

- Scattered victories become a single rights-list draft.

End hook:

- The conservative executives demand to see whether the list hides losses.

### EP022 - 기준표

3. Doyun compares it to his rights list: refrigerated route, recurring supply contract, offset workout.

Receipt:

Receipt:
